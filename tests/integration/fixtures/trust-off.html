Claims are not the same as evidence.

Every label shows the status of a fact. It does not replace your judgement.

  • Self-declared

    The company said so. Nobody independent has checked.

  • Evidence available

    Proof was provided. That alone does not make the claim verified.

  • Verified

    A defined check was completed. What it covered and its limits still matter.

  • Derived

    Worked out from other information. It is an inference, not an observed fact.

  • Outcome observed

    A real commercial event was recorded with proof. A meeting, contract, invoice and cash receipt are different events.

Trust, on every route

Every fact says how it is known.

A supplier's claim in Foshan and a buyer's interest in Manchester or Madrid get the same five labels. Each label is always an icon and a word.

  • Self-declared

    Stated by the company.

  • Evidence available

    Proof was provided.

  • Verified

    Checked by a defined process.

  • Derived

    Worked out from available data.

  • Outcome observed

    A real result was recorded.
