Brand identity v1

One mark, one name, one set of rules.

How the Corridra logo, orb, favicon and social images are used. It sits on top of the AC/1 Atlantic Cobalt design system and adds no new colour.

1. The logo family

Six parts, each with a job. Pick by space and context, never by taste.

Mark

Halftone sphere. Large brand moments, 64 px and up. mark

Orb

Derived small-size mark for favicons, avatars and the collapsed rail. orb

Wordmark

Artwork, never retyped. wordmark

Compact lockup

Product chrome: headers and footer. lockup-compact

Horizontal lockup

Wide spaces, slides, documents. lockup-horizontal

Stacked lockup

Centred or narrow spaces. lockup-stacked

2. Size ladder

The halftone dots do not survive small sizes. Below 48 px use the orb; in product chrome use the compact lockup.

3. Colorways

Cobalt is the default. Reversed is for navy only. Cobalt on navy is 2.41:1 and fails.

Cobalt

Porcelain, white, surface-subtle. The default.

Navy

Print, or where cobalt would compete with an action.

Reversed

Midnight Navy and surface-navy only.

Mono navy

One-colour print, stamps.

Mono white

On cobalt, or photos over a scrim.

Mono black

Black-only print.

5. Clear space

Keep at least X free on every side, where X is the height of the letter C in the wordmark. For the orb alone, X is a quarter of its diameter.

Asset Minimum size
Orb 16 px
Compact lockup 24 px tall
Wordmark 96 px wide
Stacked lockup 120 px wide
Horizontal lockup 160 px wide
Mark 64 px wide
Print 25 mm wide

6. Never

Each of these is a defect in review.

Stretch or squash

Rotate or skew

Shadow, glow or gradient

Old orange artwork in product

Cobalt on navy

Busy backgrounds

Dot mark below 64 px

CORRIDRA

Retyping the wordmark

Also never: use the sphere as decoration or pattern, animate the logo, change dot sizes or counts, place a tagline lockup in product UI, or generate a new logo.
