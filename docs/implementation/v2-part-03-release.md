## Contracts and limitations

See ADR-027 and the product runbook. Migration 0025 preserves previous migrations and extends existing delivery rather than replacing its services. Customer Neon identity and operator TOTP remain independent. CLIENT extensions pin exact assessments/requirements/quotes; institutional opt-in defaults false and does not expose those extensions. Capability expiry, source retention, publisher access and relationship permissions are enforced in SQL. Supplier responses remain immutable self-declarations. Historical exports retain their bytes.

Public landing pages state structural support, no announced pilot, actual customer identity/test availability and unavailable real operations. EU qualification is explicit. Intake drafts are synthetic, session-local and expire after 24 hours. All ISO company countries and controlled target markets are represented without Spain/OTHER fallback. English plus useful Bangla guidance/number-date presentation is implemented; full translated UI is not claimed. Original Chinese renders in browser, CSV and PDF through licensed embedded fonts. Document collection/download stays in the existing controlled evidence workflow. My missions currently redeems selected capabilities in memory; persistent discovery and invitation-email delivery are not introduced.
