# Part 06 requirement baseline

The governing documents are the complete constitution, public experience and implementation canvas indexed in `docs/README.md`. This packet implements Part 06 of `plan-v1.0.md`; Part 07 commercial opportunities and outcome projections remain later work.

Corridra makes cross-border market access deliberate: understand intent, research and qualify relevant counterparties, create controlled access, coordinate execution and record what happens next. The initial corridor is Bangladesh and Spain/EU. Buyers need relevance, evidence, control and an easy decline. Operators need an accountable next action. Activity, acceptance, a held meeting and cash remain different outcomes.

| Requirement                      | Implementation and acceptance                                                                                                                                                                                                                         |
| -------------------------------- | ----------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------- |
| Controlled contact               | Versioned, human-approved purpose/channel/jurisdiction policy and templates; current policy and relationship access checked before queueing and dispatch; opt-outs suppress further contact.                                                          |
| Buyer relevance and minimisation | Company, country, relevant capability, fit explanation, evidence highlights with status, uncertainty, conversation purpose and available dates only. No workspace, contacts, negotiations or evidence download capability.                            |
| No-account decisions             | Interested, Need more information, Not relevant; explicit opt-out; no mailing-list enrollment. Scoped expiring hashed links, immediate revocation, shared database budgets and atomic idempotency. GET/HEAD/prefetch never decide or consume a token. |
| Meeting truth                    | PROPOSED, ACCEPTED, SCHEDULED, HELD, DECLINED, CANCELLED, NO_SHOW and RESCHEDULE_REQUIRED enforced by services and SQL; optimistic versions, retained command history and atomic events/outbox. Acceptance has a recorded source.                     |
| Execution and continuity         | Participants, timezone-aware schedule and itinerary, operator-authored brief, observed outcome and owned/deadlined follow-up. Reject invalid times and require an explicit offset at DST ambiguity.                                                   |
| Reliable effects                 | Outbox remains transactional truth; Workflow adapter performs bounded retries with durable checkpoints, leases, stable effect IDs, dead-letter inspection and explicit retry. Deployment/retry alone never implies resending.                         |
| Trust                            | Existing MFA, country/purpose/classification permissions, relationship visibility, RLS, immutable provenance and unscanned evidence labels remain in force. No AI authority is added.                                                                 |
| Release                          | Fresh/upgrade PostgreSQL checks, negative authorization, concurrent responses, token lifecycle, DST and retry tests, public/protected browser acceptance, complete standard checks and honest release evidence.                                       |

The current runtime admits invented records only. Policy approval for synthetic/manual demonstration is not legal approval for real outreach. Actual email requires explicit send permission, reviewed jurisdiction basis and approved sender-domain/provider readiness. D08 and the scanner/account deferrals stay open until evidenced. No fabricated legal entity, contact address, customer proof or production activation is included.

Rollback disables new introductions and dispatch, revokes affected buyer links and retains history. Keep additive migrations and restore a compatible application; use forward database fixes. Pending work remains visible to operators.
