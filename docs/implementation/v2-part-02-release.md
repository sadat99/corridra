## Implemented functionality

The existing Mission OS gains reusable buyer/customer, distributor/partner, sourcing, machinery, supplier qualification, meeting/visit, fair/trip, market-validation/entry/export-access and delegation templates. Explicit installation creates immutable requirement and assessment definitions. Route availability stays in Part 1's directional packs; no seed silently approves or activates a route. Arbitrary registered routes use the same services.

Progressive requirements preserve original declaration, versioned interpretation, source/claim, author and optional privileged reviewer. Structured optional fields include product/specifications, quantities/units/MOQ/frequency, budgets/currency/basis, materials/dimensions/technical constraints, certifications/OEM/ODM, delivery/lead time/Incoterm, evidence, dates, decision criteria and responsible persons. Machinery fields cover the technical, site, acceptance, service and risk topics in the supplied Part 2 brief.

Typed target assessments retain method/version, date, responsible assessor, requirement/basis per criterion, unknowns/gaps and decision rationale. RFQs retain buyer context and intended suppliers. Original offers/revisions retain exact supplier/RFQ/requirement parentage, source/claim, prices/currency, quantity/MOQ/unit, costs, validity/lead time, Incoterm/shipping, installation/training/warranty/spares, payment terms and uncertainty. Evidence-aware comparison explicitly marks NOT SUPPLIED and NOT COMPARABLE; derived subtotals retain inputs/time without replacing original amounts. Human selections/declines and next steps append history.

Typed meetings and site visits reuse the original acceptance, scheduling, attendance and follow-up state machine. Schedule versions retain commercial destinations/events/candidates/availability/constraints/logistics and references to confirmed appointments. Commercial-origin entry and sourcing-opportunity creation are available in the existing operator workspace. Buyer-led opportunities and original financial progression/replay remain preserved. Route/job/stage/owner/overdue filtering executes before pagination.

## What users can accomplish

Exporters can retain buyer requirements and assessment decisions while continuing their existing introduction/meeting/commercial workflow. Importers and sourcing teams can request and compare original supplier offers, record samples through the original commercial service, choose/decline suppliers and request missing evidence. Machinery buyers can retain capacity, site/integration, acceptance and after-sales questions and arrange actual technical discussions or factory visits. Operators can filter missions across routes, explain qualification/selection, track missing information, schedules and owned follow-up, and observe real commercial outcomes without artificial progression.
