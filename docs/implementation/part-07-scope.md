# Part 07 requirement baseline

Historical implementation packet for its named revision. Its scope, acceptance and next-step statements are dated context. Use [the current plan](current-plan.md) and [developer handoff](../START_HERE.md) for present status; old task sequencing or unclosed-at-the-time issues are not current instructions. Do not infer full requirement acceptance from this packet.

This packet connects Corridra's existing intake, research, controlled introduction, held meeting and owned follow-up to observed commercial progress. The complete constitution, public experience and architecture canvas remain governing requirements in [the document index](../README.md). Originals and working-copy integrity are preserved.

| Requirement                | Implementation and acceptance                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                               |
| -------------------------- | ------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------- |
| Commercial origin          | An opportunity starts from a held meeting in an active follow-up/commercial mission. Mission, target, meeting, attribution statement and MISSION_ASSOCIATED scope are immutable. Existing relationship visibility applies.                                                                                                                                                                  |
| Canonical progression      | LEAD, QUALIFIED_OPPORTUNITY, PROPOSAL, COMMITMENT, CONTRACT, INVOICE, CASH_COLLECTED and RECURRING_REVENUE; explicit loss reason before cash, optimistic versions, required evidence and mandatory owner/next action/deadline. Mission qualification requires historical qualification; closure cannot overlook private unresolved opportunities. RFQ and sample remain supporting records. |
| Separate commercial truth  | Typed immutable proposal, commitment, contract, invoice and receipt records have their own source, observed date, external reference and integer minor units. A contract needs observed evidence; its reference alone proves neither signature nor payment.                                                                                                                                 |
| Accounting authority       | Operators record sales observations. Owner/admin separately confirm cash using observed accounting evidence, a human basis and fresh MFA. Overpayments, cross-currency parents, stale/restricted sources and unauthorized confirmations fail in PostgreSQL as well as the application.                                                                                                      |
| Corrections and recurrence | Exact negative compensation preserves the original and follows dependency order. Confirmed cash corrections require separate accounting confirmation. Recurrence identifies later confirmed cash from a different invoice; repeat cash is a subset of total cash.                                                                                                                           |
| Outcome projections        | Ordinary domain events, audit and outbox writes remain atomic. Minimal versioned snapshots feed synchronous relational projections. Replay is deterministic and idempotent; every authorized rebuild has an immutable reason and audit entry.                                                                                                                                               |
| Mission yield              | Actor-visible activity/access, held meetings, historical funnel attainment, distinct contract/invoice/reported/confirmed cash amounts, currency-specific totals and explicit denominators/attribution. History is paged completely; overdue meeting/opportunity actions retain owners.                                                                                                      |
| Release                    | Fresh and populated Part 06 upgrade tests, RLS/negative authority, duplicates, partial payments, mixed currencies, reversals/replay, real MFA browser intake-to-cash/recurrence and public regressions. Additive migrations 0011 to 0013 never rewrite existing records.                                                                                                                    |
