# Part 08: client and institutional delivery

Module-specific guidance. Read [the current plan](../implementation/current-plan.md), [developer handoff](../START_HERE.md), [identity recovery](neon-auth-accounts.md) and [deployment](deployment.md) for current programme/access/release status. Part-specific counts and dated rollout/settings observations below refer to their own release; inspect the current schema and executed evidence before acting. These instructions never authorize real operations or bypass readiness gates.

Use the protected operator workspace's **Client and programme delivery** panel. Programme operations are available to owner/admin/operator roles; approving publications, creating private links and publishing evidence-request instructions require owner/admin and fresh MFA. Reviewer/viewer access remains read-only. The original owner and the owner's personal admin account retain their existing permissions. This packet does not change owner-only configuration authority or enable signup.

Create a programme with a name and explicit reporting purpose. Download the intake template. The first seven headers are required: participant_reference, company, name, email, objective, market and offer. Optional fields default to attendance “Not decided”, timezone “Asia/Dhaka”, outcome “Relevant meetings”, and empty visit dates, website and notes. Multiple outcomes use a `|` separator. Participant addresses must end in `@example.invalid`; these remain synthetic self-declarations, rather than approved customer onboarding.

Preview a UTF-8 CSV or a single visible XLSX worksheet with text cells. Files are limited to 1 MiB and 100 participant rows; programmes permit 1000 participants. Formulas, macros, external relationships, XML entities, hidden content and dangerous archive expansion are rejected. Every logical row is shown, including blanks, file duplicates, existing participants and errors. Any error or changed-reference conflict blocks the entire confirmation. Review the exact counts, choose an active mission owner and explicitly acknowledge before confirming. Previews expire after one day. A request retry returns its original row results and mission identifiers; it never creates another copy. Accepted rows create the normal canonical mission records atomically through the mission service.

Review an imported or independent mission. Write the title, approved company name, objective, brief, next action and deadline intended for external delivery. Select the exact accessible meetings and opportunities, review itinerary locations and supply safe meeting labels. Financial sharing is a separate opt-in for aggregate contract, invoice and confirmed-cash amounts. The publication limits are 50 meetings and 100 opportunities. The original intake, contact details, private notes, negotiations, accounting bases and external financial references are excluded from the delivered view. A changed meeting version hides its schedule until a new publication is approved.

Select current approved publications and create a client link for one mission, or an institutional link for participants in the selected programme. Approve the exact audience, purpose, expiry and optional institutional export permission. Links expire within 30 days and also fail when a publication/link is withdrawn, a publisher loses authorization, or the relevant intake/source/evidence retention or validity ends. Reporting scopes permit 1000 approved publications, up to 3 MiB of reviewed copy and 1000 approved itinerary entries. These bounds reject an oversized scope rather than silently paging or truncating its report. The private token appears only in the URL fragment and request body; share it manually with the intended recipient. No new email is sent.

The client page shows reviewed copy, current mission progress, approved itinerary and owned evidence requests. Responses are immutable self-declarations. Supporting documents continue through the existing controlled collection, quarantine and evidence-review process. **Mark fulfilled** closes a received request without verifying its claim. Institutions receive approved programme metrics and outcomes without evidence instructions or client responses.

CSV/PDF downloads require the link's approved reporting purpose and explicit export permission. Reports contain the generation time, schema/scope, approved participant/company denominators and attribution limits. Qualified counterparties respect approved opportunity scope and active entity resolutions. Money uses the same canonical aggregation as Ops; contracts, invoices and confirmed cash stay distinct by currency, and repeat cash is already included in confirmed cash. CSV provides one row per mission with exact minor-unit columns for each supported currency and neutralizes spreadsheet formulas. PDF shapes and paginates English, Spanish and Bengali text through licensed embedded fonts. Unsupported PDF glyphs return an explicit error and preserve the complete UTF-8 CSV option. Downloads stream; artifacts are limited to 8 MiB and audited with their content hash. The same export command returns the identical artifact, while revocation/expiry still overrides a retry.

Set `CORRIDRA_DELIVERY_ENABLED=false` in the affected Vercel environment and redeploy to disable the panel and delivery/import/export endpoints. Keep the forward migrations and accepted records. Missions, meetings, original evidence and commercial operations continue through their existing interfaces. Re-enable the application switch only after the normal review and release gates; it confers no operator role or per-tenant commercial/configuration grant. Database restores, emergency ownership changes and customer rollout remain governed by the existing runbooks and deferred controls.

Part 07's production commercial write acceptance remains deferred at the owner's instruction. Keep isolated Part 08 browser acceptance separate from production read checks and from that deferred write check. Never reuse the owner's personal admin identity as an automated test account.
