# V2 commercial mission operations

**Document status: CURRENT, DATED.** The steps still describe the module. Counts, dates and rollout notes inside are historical. See the [plan status register](../PLAN_STATUS.md).

Module-specific guidance. Read [the current plan](../implementation/current-plan.md), [developer handoff](../START_HERE.md), [identity recovery](neon-auth-accounts.md) and [deployment](deployment.md) for current programme/access/release status. Part-specific counts and dated rollout/settings observations below refer to their own release; inspect the current schema and executed evidence before acting. These instructions never authorize real operations or bypass readiness gates.

Begin with the existing short synthetic mission intake and operator qualification. Preserve the customer's source declaration. Use current canonical counterparty research to add relevant targets and their source/claim records. No template requires a long public onboarding form.

An owner with fresh MFA explicitly installs the reusable definitions through `CorridorService.installTemplates` or POST `mission-templates` in the existing authenticated workspace API. Installation is idempotent. It does not approve a route or rewrite Part 1 packs. Select the requirement and assessment definitions whose schema typeKey matches the job, and append a new pack through `CorridorService.appendPack` or POST `corridor-pack`. A new registered market pair uses `createRoute` or POST `corridor-route`. All of these operations retain Part 1 authority, immutable versions and synthetic-only restrictions. Part 3 supplies broader configuration administration surfaces.

Choose an explicitly reviewed available pack and exact mission type in **Assign a reviewed route**. The existing initial BD↔CN/ES/EU packs remain drafts until explicit review. Their type availability comes from Part 1's directional seeds. Other routes use the same definitions and services. No route status establishes legal approval or supplier coverage.

In the mission card, choose **Review requirements, offers and visits**. Record objective, commercial role and product first. Expand specification/budget/evidence or machinery questions only when applicable. Budget values need original currency and quantity basis; quantities need units. Select the actual declared source and matching claim. Saving appends a requirement version and preserves the previous submission. Historical definitions cannot change an accepted submission.

Assess the target against its pinned criteria. Record each requirement, source basis and FIT/GAP/UNKNOWN/UNSUITABLE result, together with method, observation date, unknowns, gaps and rationale. A FIT result is an operator assessment, not verification. Machinery questions cover purpose, capacity, configuration, input/output, utilities, integration, documentation, commissioning, training, acceptance, warranty/spares, after-sales support, installation references, transport and risks. Obtain qualified engineering/regulatory/audit advice separately where needed.

Prepare an RFQ for exact target accounts and the current requirement. Record each original supplier response and supplied terms. Blank inputs remain NOT SUPPLIED. Monetary form inputs use original currency decimals and are converted deterministically to exact minor-unit strings. A correction appends a quotation revision; it must not move to another supplier or RFQ. Review **Original offers and revision history** when evaluating corrections. Comparison presents uncertainty and calculation scope, and does not equate incomplete or differently scoped offers. No FX conversion is assumed.

Record SELECT, DECLINE or NEED_INFORMATION with the current assessment and optional latest supplier offer, rationale, responsible operator, next action and deadline. Refresh stale requirements, route assignments, assessments or quotation revisions before making a new decision. Selection does not automatically advance a commercial funnel.

Propose a supplier meeting, factory/showroom visit, technical evaluation, institutional or trade-fair meeting from the target. Continue in **Introductions and meetings** to record actual acceptance, confirmed schedule/location/timezone, attendance and an owned follow-up. Factory review remains an operational observation. Add destinations, events, candidate sites, availability, constraints or logistics references to the versioned trip schedule. APPOINTMENT entries reference a confirmed meeting and use its actual current schedule. No booking, supplier availability or journey time is fabricated.

Record a source/claim-backed supplier response, sourcing requirement or documented RFQ as a commercial origin. Use **Create an opportunity from a sourcing observation** in existing commercial follow-through. The held-meeting path remains available for buyer-led missions. RFQ, sample, proposal, commitment, contract, invoice, observed receipt, confirmed cash and recurrence retain their original prerequisites and accounting permissions. An offer or visit does not create cash.

API reads: GET `mission-catalogue` and GET `commercial-mission?mission=<id>`. Writes: POST `commercial-mission` with requirements, assessment, rfq, quotation, decision or schedule; `meeting` with proposeActivity or existing transitions; `commercial-origin`; and the existing opportunity command. All requests require session/tenant scope, appropriate permission and same-origin POST. Execution bodies are bounded at 32 KiB; other workspace bodies remain 8 KiB. Requirements/terms have their own smaller bounds. Histories and offer displays are bounded at 100 records per mission; do not treat a bounded display as a complete financial report.

Migrate only a verified isolated synthetic branch first, then the exact approved live synthetic branch in a separate release job. Never run migrations from a build, clone customer identity data or use the customer's personal admin as a test identity. Run document/infrastructure, PostgreSQL/RLS, source/identity, commercial/financial, public/accessibility and protected operator tests. Confirm the PR's exact accepted head before normal merge/deployment. Preserve the previous compatible build and all additive data on rollback.

Keep at most ten local working branches and ten branches per managed Neon project. Check inventory before every creation and remove only proven completed temporary work. The preview and recovery scripts refuse creation when ten already exist. They do not delete unknown, live, default, sterile-parent or open-PR environments. Account-wide creation outside these tools cannot be guaranteed by a local guard. Current open dependency PRs are preserved at the owner's instruction. The owner clarified a maximum of ten total GitHub branches, including main. Complete and validate a dependency update, merge it and delete only its completed branch before publishing product work at capacity. Never discard unfinished updates. Future Dependabot limits are three npm and three Actions PRs; existing open PRs are preserved. Repository automatic branch deletion returned HTTP 403 and is not claimed active.
