# Manual data subject request runbook

Document status: CURRENT. Owned by WS-D of the commercial readiness program. Corridra handles access, correction and deletion requests by hand while legal review, the automated request process and data processing agreements are deferred by owner decision on 31 Oct 2026 (D08). This runbook is the interim process. It is not legal advice.

## Who handles requests

The account owner, using the privacy contact shown on `/privacy`. The controller is Corridra Pte Ltd, as stated on `/privacy`.

## Time limits

Reply within 30 days of receiving a request. If more time is needed, tell the person before day 30, with the reason.

## Steps

1. **Log the request** in the owner's private request log (outside the product): date received, channel, what is asked (access, correction, deletion, objection, portability), and a reference number. Do not copy the person's data into the log beyond their name and contact.
2. **Confirm identity.** Reply from the privacy contact address. Ask the person to confirm from the email address their Corridra account uses, or to sign in and send the request from their account page. Never accept a request about someone else's account without proof of authority.
3. **Find the records.** As a team owner with a fresh authenticator check:
   - Customer account and workspaces: the customer's account page and the workspace member list.
   - Requests and missions: search the mission list by company and contact.
   - Messages, meetings and reports linked to those missions.
   - Evidence files and claims linked to those missions or companies.
   - Inbox notifications and email records sent to that address.
4. **Access requests:** export what the product shows for each record (screens or the existing exports) into one file. Leave out other people's personal data and internal security details. Send it through a private link or an encrypted attachment.
5. **Correction requests:** correct the record in the product. Versioned records keep their history; add the correction as a new version with the reason "Correction requested by the data subject".
6. **Deletion requests:**
   - Close the customer account and revoke workspace memberships and private links.
   - Money and ledger records, audit events and evidence that supports a verified fact are kept where the law requires them (tax, accounting, fraud prevention). Tell the person which records are kept and why.
   - Stored files are not deleted by hand. Stored file cleanup is deferred (D06); list the files in the log so the reviewed cleanup removes them.
7. **Reply** to the person with what was done, what was kept and why, and how to complain to the data protection authority.
8. **Close the log entry** with the date and a short summary.

## What stays deferred

- Legal review of this process and of data processing agreements (D08).
- Automated export and deletion tools.
- Reviewed stored file cleanup (D06).

Risk while deferred: requests take manual work, so they are slower and easier to get wrong. Keep the log complete so nothing is missed.
