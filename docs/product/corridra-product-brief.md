### Make market entry deliberate.

Cross-border market access.

Corridra helps companies and institutions **reach the right counterparties in a new market and turn each relationship into measurable commercial progress.** Say what you want to buy, sell or organise. We find the companies that fit, show what is proven about each one, help you meet them and record what happens next.

Launch routes:

- BD and CN: sourcing and machinery
- BD and ES / EU: buyers and distributors

### Why it exists: too much activity, too little qualified access

Companies already travel, book fair stands, join delegations, buy contact lists and hire agents. The hard questions stay open.

- Who is actually worth meeting?
- Can they be reached, and do they fit?
- What happens after the meeting?
- Did any of it become business?

Finding company names is now cheap. Relevance, access, trust, local execution and follow-through are still scarce. That gap is where Corridra works.

### What it is: one mission for one commercial goal

A mission is a planned piece of work with a clear goal, market and time window. Corridra's team and platform research the market, qualify each company, prepare introductions, coordinate meetings and visits, and follow every opportunity through quotes, samples and proposals to a recorded result.

**Corridra is:** research and qualification around your goal, introductions with context, meetings and visits that answer a question, and a shared record of what followed.

**Corridra is not:** a buyer database, a fair matchmaking app, a generic CRM, a lead agency, or a travel, visa or freight service.

### How it works

1. **Say what you need.** What you want to buy, sell or organise, where and by when. No account needed to begin, and nothing is sent until you check it.
2. **Find the right companies.** We look beyond one fair, one directory or one agent's contacts, and record why each company was chosen.
3. **Review before you meet.** See why each company fits, what is proven and what is still open. Price quotes sit side by side.
4. **Meet and follow up.** Introductions, meetings and site visits, then quotes, samples and proposals, each with an owner and a date.

Intent → Market → Counterparties → Qualification → Access → Meetings and visits → Opportunity → Quote or sample → Proposal → Contract → Invoice → Cash

- **A shortlist with reasons.** Every company comes with why it fits and a label for what is proven.

A database contact is not a buyer. A meeting is not a sale. A contract is not cash.

Use cases, trust, where and when.

### Use cases

#### Meet the right knitwear buyers at a Madrid trade fair

BD → ES, export.

- **Trigger:** A Dhaka manufacturer has a fair stand and hopes buyers walk past.
- **Corridra does:** Researches Spanish distributors and retail buyers, on and off the attendee list, checks fit and proposes introductions.
- **Result:** Meetings decided before travel, and a named follow-up for each conversation.

#### Buy circular knitting machines with comparable offers

BD → CN, machinery.

- **Trigger:** A factory needs new capacity and has three quotes that cannot be compared.
- **Corridra does:** Turns the application and capacity into a specification, collects offers and shows what each includes, from freight to spares.
- **Result:** A decision on real terms, and factory visits that answer specific questions.

#### Plan a Canton Fair and factory week that pays off

BD → CN, sourcing trip.

- **Trigger:** An importer is flying to Guangzhou with a list of products and little time.
- **Corridra does:** Shortlists suppliers in Guangzhou, Foshan, Shenzhen or Yiwu and plans visits. Proposed and confirmed visits stay apart.
- **Result:** Fewer, better visits and quotes gathered in one place.

#### Test Bangladesh before opening an office

ES → BD, market entry.

- **Trigger:** A Spanish company sees demand in Bangladesh but has no local network.
- **Corridra does:** Assesses the market, finds possible customers and distributors, and arranges local checks and meetings.
- **Result:** A clear go or no-go. A well-founded "not now" also counts as a result.

#### Run a trade delegation that produces business

Programme, delegation.

- **Trigger:** A chamber is taking twelve companies abroad and is judged on outcomes, not attendance.
- **Corridra does:** Starts from each participant's own objective, builds the agenda and meetings, and keeps each company's offers and contacts private.
- **Result:** A programme report of what followed, shared with participants' consent.

#### Visit Bangladesh factories that fit the brief

EU → BD, sourcing.

- **Trigger:** A European buyer needs a new supplier and has two days in Dhaka.
- **Corridra does:** Screens factories against exact criteria and shows the reasons, the gaps and the evidence for each.
- **Result:** A shortlist worth the trip, with visits and follow-up coordinated.

### Trust you can inspect: every fact says where it comes from

| Label              | Meaning                         |
| ------------------ | ------------------------------- |
| Self-declared      | Stated by the company.          |
| Evidence available | Proof was provided.             |
| Verified           | Checked by a defined process.   |
| Derived            | Worked out from available data. |
| Outcome observed   | A real result was recorded.     |

A supplier claiming a capacity does not mean Corridra verified it. A scheduled meeting is not a held meeting. You always see the difference.

### Where and when

- **Where:** **Bangladesh ↔ China**, led by sourcing and machinery from China. **Bangladesh ↔ Spain and the EU**, led by export buyer access. Reverse directions are opened case by case. Another route? Ask, and we tell you plainly if we can help. We claim no global coverage.
- **Online:** Start a request on the web, from a phone or a laptop. The site is in English. Bangla, Chinese, Spanish, Arabic, French and German are being prepared.
- **When:** Before the trip, not after. The best moments: a China sourcing trip, the Canton Fair or an industrial show, a machinery decision, a European trade fair, a distributor search, or a delegation being planned.
- **Pricing:** Paid, scoped missions. We agree the scope and an itemised quote with you before work starts. Online payment is not available yet.

### Our commitments

- **No promised orders.** We do research, review, introductions and meetings, and record results as they happen.
- **You stay in control.** Nothing is sent on your behalf until you have checked it.
- **Private by design.** Your mission is visible only to your organisation. Delegation organisers see only what participants allow.
- **Originals kept.** Supplier quotes, currencies and documents stay as received. Translations are marked as translations.
- **People decide.** AI assistance is planned for research drafts only. It will never verify a fact, approve money or send a message for you.
