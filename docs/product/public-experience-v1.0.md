# 6. SECTION 03 — THE CONTRADICTION

### Copy

Companies spend on flights, hotels, exhibitions, delegations and management time — then arrive with too few relevant meetings and too much left to chance.

# 23. TRUST & EVIDENCE PAGE

URL:

`/trust`

## Hero

# Know what is claimed.  
# Know what is evidenced.  
# Know the difference.

---

## Copy

Cross-border decisions often rely on information collected from companies, documents, public sources, partners and field work.

Those sources do not carry equal certainty.

Corridra is designed to preserve that distinction.

---

## Evidence states

**Self-declared**

The information comes from the company/person concerned.

**Evidence available**

Supporting evidence has been provided or identified.

**Verified**

A defined verification process has been completed.

**Derived**

A conclusion or score has been inferred from available data.

**Outcome observed**

A real commercial event has occurred and been recorded.

---

## AI copy

### Heading

## AI can help Corridra reason. It cannot redefine reality.

AI may assist with:

research;

classification;

comparison;

document extraction;

matching;

summaries;

recommendations.

It does not automatically turn an inference into a fact.

Consequential actions remain subject to defined controls and, where appropriate, human review.
