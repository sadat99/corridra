## 4. Alignment flags: no guidance in the plan

Each flag has what was found, a default placement chosen by judgement under the charter, and whether the owner must call it. Defaults are proposals about labels and position. None touches data or permissions.

**A1. The customer navigation is named by the contract and locked by UI direction.** UI direction locks "Home, My Work, Companies, Results, More" and says the file is changed first, with the reason. The contract (section 4) and requirements V3-04-043 to V3-04-045 name the same set, and the register text is hash-checked, so it cannot be edited. Default: Lane 4's first commit amends the navigation table, the customer kit table and the glossary in UI direction with the owner's recorded approval. The pull request cites V3-04-043 to V3-04-045 and V3-07-019. The register is left alone and the amendment is recorded in the work log. A search for the navigation finds `tests/operator/v3-billing-ui.spec.ts`, `tests/operator/v3-service-requests-ui.spec.ts` and the capsule rule in `tests/design-system.test.mjs` (`.guide-tabs a`); Lane 4 confirms the full list before editing any of them. Owner call: confirm the new labels. Note that "Library" must keep "Results" visible as a heading, because "what results did we achieve" is one of the contract's five questions.

**A2. "Your Move", "Move card" and "Them" meet the plain-language rule.** UI direction rule 4 bans figures of speech and names "next move" as an example. The glossary says to use the real role (buyer, supplier) in interface text. The shipped email already avoids the word ("One thing needs you"), but the mission passport on a delivered report already shows "YOUR NEXT MOVE" above the staff-approved next action (`mission-passport.tsx`), so the word is in the product today. Default: keep the owner's names for the structure and in code, put every user-visible string in the catalogue so a label is one edit, and prepare a plain fallback set for the cover-the-words test: "Waiting for you" for Your Move, "Supplier" or "Buyer" for Them, "Corridra team" for Corridra, "Where this request stands" for the Road. In every language the names are translated by meaning, not literally. Owner call: yes, after the test.

**A3. Three pages already show one piece of work, with three ids.** `/account/requests/[requestId]` (request and progress), `/account/missions/[resourceId]` (the accepted Mission Room under Results, with the comparison matrix and decisions) and the plan's new `/account/rooms/[requestId]`. The plan does not mention the existing Mission Room or its mission passport (`mission-passport.tsx`: status, the approved next action and a situation-at-a-glance panel). No join between a request and a saved mission is proved. Default: the new Room is a composition above both. It is keyed by the request, shows the Road and message strip, and keeps the mission passport, and mounts the existing comparison matrix and `SavedCommercialReport` as the Compare and Evidence lenses only where a readable link between the two exists. Where none exists it links out and invents no join. Both old URLs keep working. Lane 1's inventory states the link or its absence. Owner call: no.

**A4. Three owned parts are not mentioned.** `JourneyBar` (UI direction: "work items show the journey bar", used today only in the request flow), `NextStepCard` (locked: one per screen, first on Home and on a piece of work; used today only on the account home) and `FactLabel` (the five trust names with icon and plain line). Default: the Road is the work-item journey bar. It reuses the same list, `aria-current` and done markers and does not edit `journey-bar.tsx`. The first move on the front door is the next-step card. The trust seal is `FactLabel`, not a second set of five silhouettes and glosses. Owner call: no.

**A5. The Road mapping runs backward and cannot light every step.** The legal path is MEETINGS, FOLLOW_UP, COMMERCIAL_PIPELINE, CLOSED, and COMMERCIAL_PIPELINE can also be reached from ACTIVE or TARGETING (`packages/missions/src/index.ts`, `missionCommand`). The plan maps FOLLOW_UP to step 6 and COMMERCIAL_PIPELINE to step 5, so the marker would step back. No status means Compare or Decide. ON_HOLD stores `held_from`, which the customer cannot read, so the marker position is unknown. Default for ADR-0067: DRAFT and QUALIFICATION to Define; ACTIVE, TARGETING and OUTREACH to Find; MEETINGS to Visit; FOLLOW_UP, COMMERCIAL_PIPELINE and CLOSED to Follow through, each with a plain sub-line taken from the status; ON_HOLD with no marker and a side note; CANCELLED as a side note. Compare and Decide light only when a readable record for the same piece of work exists (a delivered report, a reviewed decision disclosure; customers cannot record a decision, they read reviewed ones), and otherwise read "not tracked yet". A property test over the transition graph proves the step never decreases. Owner call: confirm at ADR-0067.

**A6. The holder badge cannot be derived as planned.** The plan derives it from status and who wrote the latest reply. Replies are team-only, so "You" and "Them" can never come from them. Default: in Phase 1 the Road carries no holder badge. A holder appears where it is true by construction: on front door cards for items the person holds, and on service request rows where the status says who waits. "Them" is shown as Supplier or Buyer and arrives with Move records and the Reply Page. Test participants are told this before the cover-the-words test. Owner call: no.

**A7. Workspace and role scope is not mentioned.** A person can belong to several customer workspaces with different roles (workspace chooser, member roles, leaving). There is no active-workspace state today: no cookie or storage, the workspace is the address. A person's requests belong to the person, and several actions are only for a workspace owner or admin. Default: the front door is person-level and invents no active workspace. Each card names its workspace when the person has more than one, and an action appears only for a role that may take it. Sample or real mode is read per tenant. Owner call: no.

**A8. The planned front door has no start action.** The account home today leads with a next-step card to start or continue a request. The plan's empty state shows only the latest mission.
