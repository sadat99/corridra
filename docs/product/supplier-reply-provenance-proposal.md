# Staff transcription of supplier replies

**Document status: WAITING OWNER.** Decision: PROPOSED, 29 October 2026. No implementation is authorised by this note.

A staff member must not appear to be the supplier. ADR069 leaves this workflow unbuilt until provenance is decided.

Options: (1) keep supplier-only submission; (2) let staff transcribe an identified source message and require the supplier to approve it before it becomes a supplier reply; (3) publish a staff statement immediately, explicitly attributed to staff and never counted as a supplier answer.

Recommendation: option 2. Keep the immutable original message reference, supplier identity and permission, transcribing staff identity/time, exact original wording, any translation as a separate attributed version, and supplier approval identity/time. Until approval, it is a staff draft, excluded from supplier-response and evidence claims. Existing source rights, supplier-link scope and expiry must still apply. Retention and access need a privacy review before storing source attachments.

Owner question: may staff prepare an attributed transcription that becomes a supplier reply only after the supplier explicitly approves the exact text?

No schema, status, reply path or permission changes are made here.
