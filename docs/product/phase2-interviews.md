# Phase 2 interview questions

**Document status: WAITING OWNER.** Interview design for the owner; nothing is built because of it. See the [plan status register](../PLAN_STATUS.md).

Status: design note for the owner, 24 October 2026. Ten to fifteen conversations decide whether the decision card ([ADR-070](../adr/0070-decision-card-and-sharing.md)) and Pulse email ([ADR-067](../adr/0067-notification-records-and-pulse.md)) are worth switching on. Nothing here is built because of an answer; each answer only changes what the owner enables.

## Who to ask

Ten to fifteen customers from the people who have sent or could send a request: owners of small importers and exporters, people who buy machines, and people who organise trade groups. At least four in Bangladesh and at least four who read Bangla first. At least three where a co-owner, a family member or a bank takes part in decisions.

## Questions

1. Think of the last time you bought from or sold to a business in another country. Who else had to agree before you went ahead? What did you show them?
2. How did you show them: a call, a message, a screenshot, a printed page? What was missing from it?
3. Would you want to send a read-only page about one decision to that person? What must it never show them?
4. For how long should such a page work? What should happen when you stop it?
5. Has a bank asked you for papers about a deal? What did it ask for, and how long did it take?
6. When you send us a request, what do you check afterwards, and how often? Where do you look first?
7. What would make you open an email from us the same day? What would make you ignore it?
8. Which of these would you want by email: something needs me, my request moved forward, a date is close, my request is finished? Which would you not want?
9. Would you rather get one email a day or each message when it happens? Which hours should we never email you in?
10. Which language do you want our emails in? Is English enough for the date and the title?
11. If an email said only the name of your request and a date, and you had to sign in to see more, would that be enough, or annoying?
12. Who in your company should receive these emails: only you, or others too?
13. When the team asks you for one thing, what stops you from doing it quickly?
14. Have you ever muted or unsubscribed from a business message? Why?
15. If we offered these two things next month, which one would you use first?

## What the answers decide

| Answer pattern                                                   | Owner action                                                            |
| ---------------------------------------------------------------- | ----------------------------------------------------------------------- |
| Co-owners or banks take part in more than a third of decisions   | Build the decision card; set its default expiry                         |
| People already check the site first and rarely open mail         | Keep Pulse to "something needs me" only, with a daily digest by default |
| Bangla readers want Bangla mail and will not read English titles | Do not turn Pulse on for them before the Bangla reviewer signs off      |
| People want mail but not at night                                | Keep quiet hours on by default                                          |
