# ADR-021: Commercial funnel, cash and outcome projections

Status: accepted for Part 07 implementation. Covers canvas ADR topic 006. Release acceptance is recorded separately.

Opportunities belong to an existing held meeting, target and mission, with immutable origin attribution and an explicit attribution statement. The relationship's visibility and writing rules continue through every commercial record. Owners, next actions and deadlines remain mandatory. The funnel stays LEAD, QUALIFIED_OPPORTUNITY, PROPOSAL, COMMITMENT, CONTRACT, INVOICE, CASH_COLLECTED and RECURRING_REVENUE; LOST is allowed before cash, with an explicit reason. RFQ and sample are supporting records, not new stages. Historical stage attainment does not prove that current net cash remains positive after correction.

One append-only, typed commercial-record table stores RFQ, SAMPLE, PROPOSAL, COMMITMENT, CONTRACT, INVOICE, CASH_RECEIPT and REPEAT_ORDER records. Composite foreign keys and guards enforce the exact opportunity, parent kind, dates and currencies. SQL views expose the canonical record families. A contract requires a commitment, an invoice requires a contract, and a receipt requires its invoice. Each record has a source, observed date, author, reason and optional available evidence object. Claims and evidence are displayed separately from accounting confirmation. No payment API, external signature or guessed payment verification is introduced.

Money is a decimal string of integer minor units, converted only through PostgreSQL bigint/numeric and JavaScript BigInt. Initial supported ISO currencies are BDT, EUR, USD, GBP and JPY with recorded exponents; no FX conversion or combined currency total is offered. Document and receipt amounts do not become cash until an owner/admin with fresh MFA appends an accounting confirmation and its observation source. Accounting observations are distinct from sales assertions, including when a small operator team uses the same human in both roles. Operators and AI cannot confirm cash. A later accounting role can replace this initial owner/admin authority through an explicit permission decision.

Financial corrections append one exact full compensating record referencing the original. Original amounts are never edited. Correct the dependent cash first, then invoice, then contract. A cash reversal must itself receive accounting confirmation; totals include it only after confirmation. Replacements are new records with new evidence and command identities. Recurrence links a later confirmed receipt from a distinct invoice to an earlier confirmed receipt in the same currency; repeat cash is a subset of cash and is never added to it again.

Commercial commands serialize per tenant, use expected opportunity versions, and retain an immutable actor/request-hash/result ledger. Both SQL and application permissions enforce transitions, active owners, purpose, country, classification, relationship visibility and source retention. The opportunities.commands flag defaults off. Turning it off blocks commands in SQL as well as routes; historical reads remain available. Mission commands separately enable FOLLOW_UP to COMMERCIAL_PIPELINE and closure after commercial outcomes and open follow-ups are resolved. The commercial module never writes mission or meeting tables.

Ordinary opportunity, record and accounting mutations emit immutable domain events, audit and outbox records atomically. A database trigger captures a versioned, minimal commercial outcome snapshot referencing that domain event. Snapshots contain identifiers, state, dates, currency and minor units, not private source text or contacts. Synchronous relational projections apply each immutable snapshot once inside the same transaction. No external queue or graph database is needed. Owner/admin rebuild with fresh MFA serializes with commands and replays retained snapshots deterministically. Derived tables accept no direct runtime mutations. Projections retain per-opportunity and per-record grain so dashboard joins do not multiply amounts.

Mission yield separates research activity, buyer access, observed meetings, historical funnel attainment, document totals, reported receipts, confirmed net cash and recurrence. Counts have explicit denominators and only include rows the current actor may read. Money remains grouped by currency, mission and attribution scope. Historical stage and correction labels remain visible. Overdue meeting follow-ups and opportunity next actions have owners and deadlines.

The runtime remains synthetic with existing legal, retention, scanner and pilot deferrals. Customer signup remains disabled under D09. No real outreach or customer-data activation follows from this implementation. Rollback disables opportunities.commands, preserves additive schema and immutable records, and uses traceable compensation and projection replay for repairs.
