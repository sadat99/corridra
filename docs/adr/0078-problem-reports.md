# ADR-078: Problem reports (Help > Report a Problem)

Status: Proposed, 10 November 2026, for the owner's decisions of that day in session S17 ("Build a form later: a real in-app problem report with staff review" and "3, now"; the one-off exception to navigation-v1 binding 7 for this item only; the existing privacy notice line "Messages you send us (support and privacy requests)" covers it; customers with no workspace may file). An optional, additive database module that the owner merges and installs. No application switch, no email, no upload, no AI.

## Context

The customer and team Help menus show "Report a Problem" as Coming soon (`apps/web/lib/ux/customer-navigation.ts`, `apps/web/lib/ux/team-help.ts`). Nothing in the repository records a problem a person reports. The nearest existing record is the erasure request of the account lifecycle module (`packages/db/customer-account-lifecycle-migrations/`): append-only, a reference, staff status rows with a note the person reads, idempotent commands, a staff authority with a fresh authenticator check and a sample or real split of the staff queue. A problem report needs the same shape, but it is a support message, not a legal request: the V3 contract forbids merging independent legal and privacy authorities for convenience, so the erasure tables and the erasure staff authority are the pattern only.

Three facts constrain the design. Customer and staff identities are separate (ADR-032): a customer proof never yields a staff actor and the reverse. Some customers have no workspace, so a report cannot always borrow a workspace for its side or for an event chain. The install workflow already has the 25 manual inputs GitHub allows (KI-25), so the module cannot have its own input.

## Decision

1. **Module.** `packages/db/customer-problem-report-migrations/`, registry `customer_problem_report_migrations`, installer `migrateCustomerProblemReports` in `packages/db/src/customer-problem-report-migrate.ts`, which first runs `migrateCustomerAccountLifecycle` (it depends on the closed-account gate, the erasure tables and the erasure scope function) and refuses a changed or missing applied file. The owner installs it through the existing `field_modules_action` input with a new choice `problem_reports` (script variable `PROBLEM_REPORTS_ACTION=install`). Installing is the activation: there is no control table and no application switch. Everything is additive: new tables and new functions only; no existing function, policy, grant or row changes.
2. **Records.** In `customer_private`, with row level security on, every privilege revoked from `PUBLIC`, `corridra_runtime` and `corridra_auth`, and `app.reject_rewrite()` on update and delete:
   - `problem_report`: id, a number and the generated reference `RP-nnnnnn`, the reporter kind (`customer` or `team`) with exactly one of a customer principal (`customer_private.principal`) or a staff principal plus the staff workspace it was filed in (`identity.principal`, `app.tenant`), the side frozen at filing (`sample` or `real`), the category (`did_not_work`, `could_not_find`, `words_wrong`, `something_else`), the command id (unique per reporter) and the filing time.
   - `problem_report_words`: what the reporter wrote: "This page" with a sanitised path, or "Another page" with a short plain text, and the optional detail (0 to 1000 plain characters, at least 10 for Something Else). Never updated. A row may be deleted only when an erasure row for its report exists (decision 6).
   - `problem_report_status_event`: received, in_review, resolved, closed; the moves received to in_review, received to closed, in_review to resolved or closed; resolved and closed are final and carry a staff note of 10 to 1000 plain characters that the reporter reads; each move carries the expected state and a unique command id, and records the staff principal and the staff workspace that moved it.
   - `problem_report_erasure`: one row per report whose words were removed under an erasure request.
3. **Plain text and paths.** The shared plain-text rule (`packages/delivery/src/customer-service-staff.ts`, moved unchanged to `packages/domain` so both packages use one copy) is enforced at the route and again by the database: no control characters, no `://`, `@` or `www.`. Paths keep only the pathname, drop query and fragment, turn uuid segments into `:id`, are at most 200 characters and must start with the reporter's own shell (`/account` for customers, `/operator` for the team); anything else is filed as Another page.
4. **Who files and reads.** Customers through `customer.*` functions keyed on `customer.actor_id()`; team members (owner, admin, operator, reviewer or viewer of the selected staff workspace, live session, sample workspace or a real one while real use is allowed, no fresh authenticator check) through `app.*` functions keyed on `app.actor_id()` and `app.member_role()`. Neither path accepts a person id. A reporter reads only their own reports (a team member: only those filed in the selected workspace); any other reference answers exactly as an unknown one. A closed customer account cannot file and can still read. At most 10 reports per person per rolling 24 hours, counted from the person's own report rows (SQLSTATE `54000`, answered as 429 with the shared limit words); a replayed command is answered before the count. Commands are idempotent by (reporter, command id); the same id with a different body is a conflict.
5. **Staff triage and the sample or real split.** Owners and admins of a staff workspace with a fresh authenticator check, through a new staff authority function with the same checks as `customer_private.staff_privacy_actor()` but its own name and words, so the support and privacy authorities stay independent. Staff see the reference, reporter kind, filing time, category, path or page text, detail, history and side, never a name, email or workspace content. Team reports are triaged only in the staff workspace they were filed in. A customer report's side is fixed at filing: real when the person has a real workspace, else sample when they have any workspace, else the deployment's state (real when `customer_private.real_use_configuration` is enabled, otherwise sample). As with erasure requests (account module 0004), a report later moves to real when its reporter gains a real workspace and never moves back. A customer report is real once its reporter has ever held a membership or invited membership in a real workspace; those rows are immutable, so the side never moves back, unlike the present-tense erasure scope. Staff of a sample workspace see only sample reports; staff of a real workspace only real ones; a report on the other side is not listed and answers as not found. The rule is a single expression, so every report is on exactly one side.
6. **Erasure covers reports.** A staff function, under the erasure authority (`staff_privacy_actor` and `erasure_in_scope`), given an erasure request that is in progress or completed, writes an erasure row for each of that person's customer reports and deletes their words. The reference, category, times, status history and staff notes stay; the reporter's list shows that the words were removed. Team reports are staff data and outside the customer erasure request.
7. **Events.** None. The append-only report, status and erasure rows are the audit record (who, when, what, why). Nothing is delivered, so nothing goes to the outbox, and a customer without a workspace has no tenant for an event chain.
8. **Availability.** The application treats the module as present when the catalogue shows its customer command function (the probe pattern of `packages/auth/src/customer-erasure-queue.ts`: a present module is remembered, a missing one is checked again after a minute). Until then both Help menus keep "Report a Problem" as Coming soon and both routes answer 503 "not available yet". Team Help links to a team page, never to `/account`.

## Consequences

- Customers and team members can tell the Corridra team about a problem and follow its status without email. Owners and admins answer from a panel inside the existing settings section, so the 18 staff sections stay 18.
- Two new staff permissions in the existing matrix (`packages/domain/src/index.ts`): `problems.report` for all five roles and `problems.triage` for owner and admin with the fresh authenticator check. Removing words under an erasure request uses the erasure queue's existing permission.
- Rollback is a revert of the application change: the menus return to Coming soon and the routes go away. The module stays installed and its records stay; there is no down migration and no way to pause filing without a deploy (see Rejected options).
- The side split depends on staff of a real workspace reaching the panel, which today needs `CORRIDRA_REAL_USE_ENABLED=true` (KI-41). Real reports filed while no real staff can reach the panel wait unseen.
- Counting in-scope reports evaluates the side rule per row. That is fine at support volumes; if it ever is not, a forward migration can store the moves to real.

## Rejected options

- **Reuse the erasure tables or `staff_privacy_actor` for triage.** Merges a legal and a support authority (V3 contract) and would let a later change to one silently change the other.
- **A control table and an install `enable` and `pause`, or a new application switch.** Lets filing pause without a deploy, but adds an owner step and a switch the owner did not ask for, for a low-risk, rate-limited, text-only form. The owner can still ask for it; it would be a forward migration.
- **A domain event and outbox row per report.** Needs a tenant the no-workspace customer does not have, and nothing consumes it.
- **Counting in `app.intake_rate_limit`.** A second counter beside the exact count of the person's own append-only rows, with fixed windows instead of a rolling 24 hours.
- **One identity column for both reporter kinds.** Would blur customer and staff identities (ADR-032); two typed columns with a check keep them apart.
- **A new `field_modules_action`-independent workflow input.** Impossible: the workflow is at GitHub's 25-input limit (KI-25).
- **Keeping the words forever.** Append-only words could not honour an erasure request.
