# ADR-078: Problem reports (Help > Report a Problem)

Status: Proposed, 10 November 2026, for the owner's decisions of that day in session S17 ("Build a form later: a real in-app problem report with staff review" and "3, now"; the one-off exception to navigation-v1 binding 7 for this item only; the existing privacy notice line "Messages you send us (support and privacy requests)" covers it; customers with no workspace may file). An optional, additive database module that the owner merges and installs. No application switch, no email, no upload, no AI.

## Context

The customer and team Help menus show "Report a Problem" as Coming soon (`apps/web/lib/ux/customer-navigation.ts`, `apps/web/lib/ux/team-help.ts`). Nothing in the repository records a problem a person reports. The nearest existing record is the erasure request of the account lifecycle module (`packages/db/customer-account-lifecycle-migrations/`): append-only, a reference, staff status rows with a note the person reads, idempotent commands, a staff authority with a fresh authenticator check and a sample or real split of the staff queue. A problem report needs the same shape, but it is a support message, not a legal request: the V3 contract forbids merging independent legal and privacy authorities for convenience, so the erasure tables and the erasure staff authority are the pattern only.

Three facts constrain the design. Customer and staff identities are separate (ADR-032): a customer proof never yields a staff actor and the reverse. Some customers have no workspace, so a report cannot always borrow a workspace for its side or for an event chain. The install workflow already has the 25 manual inputs GitHub allows (KI-25), so the module cannot have its own input.

## Decision

1. **Module.** `packages/db/customer-problem-report-migrations/`, registry `customer_problem_report_migrations`, installer `migrateCustomerProblemReports` in `packages/db/src/customer-problem-report-migrate.ts`, which first runs `migrateCustomerAccountLifecycle` (it depends on the closed-account gate, the erasure tables and the erasure scope function) and refuses a changed or missing applied file. The owner installs it through the existing `field_modules_action` input with a new choice `problem_reports` (script variable `PROBLEM_REPORTS_ACTION=install`). Installing is the activation: there is no control table and no application switch. Everything is additive: new tables and new functions only; no existing function, policy, grant or row changes in 0001. Migration 0002 (decision 3) and migration 0003 (decision 6) each replace named functions behind a guard that compares the live function with the exact text it replaces, and the three files are never edited once merged.
2. **Records.** In `customer_private`, with row level security on, every privilege revoked from `PUBLIC`, `corridra_runtime` and `corridra_auth`, and `app.reject_rewrite()` on update and delete:
   - `problem_report`: id, a number and the generated reference `RP-nnnnnn`, the reporter kind (`customer` or `team`) with exactly one of a customer principal (`customer_private.principal`) or a staff principal plus the staff workspace it was filed in (`identity.principal`, `app.tenant`), the side frozen at filing (`sample` or `real`), the category (`did_not_work`, `could_not_find`, `words_wrong`, `something_else`), the command id (unique per reporter) and the filing time.
   - `problem_report_words`: what the reporter wrote: "This page" with a sanitised path, or "Another page" with a short plain text, and the optional detail (0 to 1000 plain characters, at least 10 for Something Else). Never updated. A row may be deleted only when an erasure row or a self-removal row for its report exists (decision 6).
   - `problem_report_status_event`: received, in_review, resolved, closed; the moves received to in_review, received to closed, in_review to resolved or closed; resolved and closed are final and carry a staff note of 10 to 1000 plain characters that the reporter reads; each move carries the expected state and a unique command id, and records the staff principal and the staff workspace that moved it.
   - `problem_report_erasure`: one row per report whose words were removed under an erasure request.
   - `problem_report_self_removal` (migration 0003): one row per customer report whose words the reporter removed themselves (decision 6). It names the report, the person and the command, holds no words, is never updated or deleted, and cannot name a team report or another person's report. It is a separate table from `problem_report_erasure`, so the two authorities keep separate records.
3. **Plain text and paths.** The shared plain-text rule (`packages/delivery/src/customer-service-staff.ts`, moved to `packages/domain` in PR2 with the wider set below, so both packages use one copy) is enforced at the route and again by the database: no control characters, no `://`, `@` or `www.`. The exact rule: the database refuses U+0001 to U+001F and U+007F (as 0001 did) and, from migration 0002, also the C1 controls U+0080 to U+009F, U+2028, U+2029, the bidi override and isolate characters U+202A to U+202E and U+2066 to U+2069, plus `://`, `@` and `www.`. The route's shared rule must refuse exactly the same set, so a person never sees the database refuse what the form accepted. Rows already stored are not re-checked. Paths keep only the pathname, drop query and fragment, turn uuid segments into `:id`, are at most 200 characters and must start with the reporter's own shell (`/account` for customers, `/operator` for the team); anything else is filed as Another page.
4. **Who files and reads.** Customers through `customer.*` functions keyed on `customer.actor_id()`; team members (owner, admin, operator, reviewer or viewer of the selected staff workspace, live session, sample workspace or a real one while real use is allowed, no fresh authenticator check) through `app.*` functions keyed on `app.actor_id()` and `app.member_role()`. Neither path accepts a person id. A reporter reads only their own reports (a team member: only those filed in the selected workspace); any other reference answers exactly as an unknown one. A closed customer account cannot file and can still read and remove its own words (decision 6, as amended). At most 10 reports per person per rolling 24 hours, counted from the person's own report rows (SQLSTATE `54000`, answered as 429 with the shared limit words); a replayed command is answered before the count. Commands are idempotent by (reporter, command id); the same id with a different body is a conflict.
5. **Staff triage and the sample or real split.** Owners and admins of a staff workspace with a fresh authenticator check, through a new staff authority function with the same checks as `customer_private.staff_privacy_actor()` but its own name and words, so the support and privacy authorities stay independent. Staff see the reference, reporter kind, filing time, category, path or page text, detail, history and side, never a name, email or workspace content. Team reports are triaged only in the staff workspace they were filed in. A customer report's side is fixed at filing: real when the person has a real workspace, else sample when they have any workspace, else the deployment's state (real when `customer_private.real_use_configuration` is enabled, otherwise sample). Unlike erasure requests (account module 0004, whose scope is present-tense), a report later moves to real when its reporter gains a real workspace and never moves back. A customer report is real once its reporter has ever held a membership or invited membership in a real workspace; those rows are immutable, so the side never moves back. Staff of a sample workspace see only sample reports; staff of a real workspace only real ones; a report on the other side is not listed and answers as not found. The rule is a single expression, so every report is on exactly one side.
6. **Erasure covers reports.** A staff function, under the erasure authority (`staff_privacy_actor` and `erasure_in_scope`), given an erasure request that is in progress or completed, writes an erasure row for each of that person's customer reports, up to the bound below, and deletes their words. The reference, category, times, status history and staff notes stay; the reporter's list shows that the words were removed. Team reports are staff data and outside the customer erasure request.
   - **What the removal covers.** It removes the words of the person's customer reports filed at or before the erasure request's completed time (while the request is in progress, at or before the moment of the call). A report filed later needs a new erasure request. A retry of a command that removed words answers with its original count; a command that removed none runs again; the same command id used under another request is a conflict. Migration 0002 of this module (`packages/db/customer-problem-report-migrations/0002_*.sql`) adds this time bound and this conflict to the function that 0001 created; 0001 itself removed the words of every customer report of the person that still had them, and did not refuse a reused command id under another request. One limit was known until migration 0003: a report filed before the cutoff whose transaction commits after the removal scan was missed by that run and by a retry of the same command (a new command id after the request is completed still covered it, because the bound is the completed time). It is closed from 0003 for new removals: the staff removal takes the per-person report lock that filing also holds until it commits, so a filing and a removal are seen one after the other.
   - **Who cannot hold an erasure request.** Three gaps, stated plainly. (1) A customer with no workspace cannot hold one: the account module raises "There is no workspace on this account" (`packages/db/customer-account-lifecycle-migrations/0002_account_chain_and_undo_words.sql:39`). (2) Nor can a person whose only link is a revoked membership and who has no `account_chain` row. (3) No one can file one while the account lifecycle control is closed or paused: on a database that never opened it, such as straight after a first install of this module, or after a `lifecycle_pause`. Installing this module writes no control row and does not close anything: the control is opened only by a separate offline operation, and a control that is already open stays open. In production gap 3 does not apply today: the ledger (`docs/V3_LEDGER.md`) records the G4 and G5 controls as enabled on 7 November 2026 by install 42364802587 and still open (`lifecycle_pause` not run), and KI-41 (`docs/v3/KNOWN_ISSUES.md`) says the 7 November enable stands and was not changed since. The production switch values (`CORRIDRA_REAL_USE_ENABLED`, which the real-side staff path in KI-41 needs) are not observed. Gaps 1 and 2 remain everywhere for the staff path. Where a gap holds, staff cannot remove the words; the reporter can, through the self-removal path below, which none of the three gaps stops.
   - **Owner decision (10 November 2026, 15:50:20Z, as recorded in the ledger), for these reporters.** The owner answered "A": "A. Build an erasure path for them (Recommended): Fuller privacy: a small follow-up lets these reporters ask for their words to be removed. PR2 merges now; the follow-up comes before customers hear about the feature." The order that follows: PR2 (the module, filing, reading and triage) merges first; the follow-up (PR2b: migration 0003, its application check and this amendment) merges and is installed before PR3, which adds the screen links, and before customers hear of the feature. Customer-facing words for the removal are written with PR3 and reviewed by the owner; PR2b adds no customer copy.
   - **Self-removal path (migration 0003).** A customer-only function, `customer.problem_report_remove_own_words(command)`, keyed on `customer.actor_id()` alone (ADR-032): no person id, report id or erasure request is accepted, a staff session or no session is refused, and `corridra_auth` has no execute right. It is the same authority as filing and reading one's own reports, so it is separate from the erasure authority on purpose (`staff_privacy_actor`, `erasure_in_scope` and the erasure tables are not used by it, and the V3 contract forbids merging the two for convenience).
     - **What it covers.** Every customer report of the signed-in person that still has words, in one call, oldest first (no choice per report). It deletes only the words. The reference, category, filing time, side, status history and staff notes stay, exactly as in the staff removal, and the list and detail show the words as removed. A report filed after the call keeps its words and needs a new call. Team reports are outside it (staff data), and the table that records it cannot name one.
     - **No lifecycle gate.** It never asks the account lifecycle control, so it works while that control is closed, paused or was never opened, and for a person who has no workspace or only a revoked membership (so no `account_chain` row). A closed account can use it, inside the undo window and after it: closing an account does not stop the customer session (`customer.actor_id()` ignores closure), and the route does not apply the closed-account gate to it (the same `allowClosedAccount` access the reads use). Only a disabled person is refused.
     - **Replay and no-op.** The same command by the same person answers with the first count and writes nothing (`changed` is false). A call that finds nothing to remove writes nothing, so the same command runs again after a later filing. Another person using the same command id acts only on their own reports.
     - **One lock, one record.** It takes the same per-person report lock as filing (`customer.problem_report:` plus the person); the staff removal takes it right after its lifecycle lock, so the order is always the lifecycle lock, then the report lock, and a staff removal and a self-removal are seen one after the other. The staff removal skips a report that has a self-removal row, so nothing is counted twice, and a staff removal followed by a self-removal removes nothing more. A person who later gains a workspace stays removed. Each removed report gets one `problem_report_self_removal` row, which the words guard needs before it lets the delete through; every update and every unrecorded delete still fails. No event is written (decision 7).
     - **Irreversible by design.** The words are deleted and cannot be restored; the self-removal row holds no words.
   - **Known limit: staff notes survive removal.** A staff note is staff text the reporter reads and it stays after the words are removed (above). If staff copy the reporter's words into a note, those words survive. Staff should not quote them; PR3 gives staff a hint line to that effect (wording for owner review).
   - **Cross-side routing.** A revoked real member whose account chain is a sample workspace has their erasure request routed to sample privacy staff, because the account module's scope is present-tense (existing behaviour). Sample staff therefore remove the words of a report that stays on the real side.
7. **Events.** None. The append-only report, status and erasure rows are the audit record (who, when, what, why). Nothing is delivered, so nothing goes to the outbox, and a customer without a workspace has no tenant for an event chain.
8. **Availability.** The application treats the module as present when the catalogue shows its customer command function (the probe pattern of `packages/auth/src/customer-erasure-queue.ts`: a present module is remembered, a missing one is checked again after a minute). Until then both Help menus keep "Report a Problem" as Coming soon and both routes answer 503 "not available yet". Team Help links to a team page, never to `/account`. The removal of a customer's own words has its own, separate check (`selfRemovalAvailable` in `packages/auth/src/problem-reports.ts`): the function `customer.problem_report_remove_own_words(uuid)` exists and the stored body of `customer.problem_report_remove_words` names `problem_report_self_removal` (read from `pg_proc.prosrc`, the same on every PostgreSQL major version, with the same caching). It gates only that removal (503 "not available yet" until it holds); filing, reading and triage never wait for migration 0003, so a deployment on 0001 and 0002 keeps working. The screens (PR3) offer the customer removal only when both checks hold.

## Consequences

- Customers and team members can tell the Corridra team about a problem and follow its status without email. Owners and admins answer from a panel inside the existing settings section, so the 18 staff sections stay 18.
- Two new staff permissions in the existing matrix (`packages/domain/src/index.ts`): `problems.report` for all five roles and `problems.triage` for owner and admin with the fresh authenticator check. Removing words under an erasure request uses the erasure queue's existing permission. A customer removing their own words needs no staff permission and no new switch, and the matrix is unchanged.
- Rollback is a revert of the application change: the menus return to Coming soon and the routes go away. The module stays installed and its records stay; there is no down migration and no way to pause filing without a deploy (see Rejected options).
- The side split depends on staff of a real workspace reaching the panel, which today needs `CORRIDRA_REAL_USE_ENABLED=true` (KI-41). Real reports filed while no real staff can reach the panel wait unseen.
- Counting in-scope reports evaluates the side rule per row. That is fine at support volumes; if it ever is not, a forward migration can store the moves to real.

## Rejected options

- **Reuse the erasure tables or `staff_privacy_actor` for triage.** Merges a legal and a support authority (V3 contract) and would let a later change to one silently change the other.
- **A control table and an install `enable` and `pause`, or a new application switch.** Lets filing pause without a deploy, but adds an owner step and a switch the owner did not ask for, for a low-risk, rate-limited, text-only form. The owner can still ask for it; it would be a forward migration.
- **A domain event and outbox row per report.** Needs a tenant the no-workspace customer does not have, and nothing consumes it.
- **Counting in `app.intake_rate_limit`.** A second counter beside the exact count of the person's own append-only rows, with fixed windows instead of a rolling 24 hours.
- **One identity column for both reporter kinds.** Would blur customer and staff identities (ADR-032); two typed columns with a check keep them apart.
- **A new `field_modules_action`-independent workflow input.** Impossible: the workflow is at GitHub's 25-input limit (KI-25).
- **For the reporters the erasure path cannot reach: loosen `problem_report_erasure`, add account-level erasure, or add a staff step under a new authority.** Loosening the erasure authority merges two independent privacy authorities (V3 contract); account-level erasure is a different and larger product; a staff step would make a reporter wait for staff to delete their own words. A customer-only function on the customer's own identity is the smallest path that needs neither.
- **Keeping the words forever.** Append-only words could not honour an erasure request. The words can be removed only for people who can hold an erasure request: not a customer with no workspace, not a person whose only link is a revoked membership with no `account_chain` row, and not anyone while the account lifecycle control is closed or paused (a database that never opened it, or after a `lifecycle_pause`; in production the controls have been open since install 42364802587 on 7 November 2026 and have not been paused, so that gap does not apply there today, though the production switch values, including `CORRIDRA_REAL_USE_ENABLED` for the real-side staff path in KI-41, are not observed; the first two gaps remain everywhere; installing this module neither opens nor closes the control). Decision 6 records the owner's answer for those reporters: the self-removal path.
