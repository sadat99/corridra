# ADR-053: Programme terms, eligibility and places for independent organisers

Status: bounded implementation, synthetic only, default off (`CORRIDRA_CUSTOMER_PROGRAMME_TERMS_ENABLED`). V3-06-005, V3-06-006 and V3-06-007 stay PARTIAL for the whole product. This decision lets an organiser say who may take part, what participants agree to and how many places there are, and lets a participant apply and accept. It sends no message, moves no money and verifies nothing.

The V3 contract asks an organiser to define "eligibility, capacity and participation terms" (journey step 2), to "review eligibility, confirmations and participation status" (step 6), to assign responsibilities and coordination tasks (step 9) and to see capacity and participant satisfaction (organiser economics). Registration (ADR-041) is deliberately self-declared and "not eligibility confirmation", capacity (ADR-047) is "a plan, not a gate", and tasks (ADR-047) carry only a free-text assignee label. This module adds the missing decisions on top of those, without changing them.

## Audit

Reused unchanged: programmes and their ownership command (ADR-037), invitations, consented registrations and withdrawals (ADR-041, `participant_scope`, `coordination_registration_state`), canonical context (ADR-043), organisation profiles (ADR-044, read only), the economics ledger (ADR-046, read only), coordination tasks, capacity record, feedback and report (ADR-047), templates (ADR-048) and member roles (ADR-051, `workspace_member_role_now`).

Already present and therefore not rebuilt: participant satisfaction (one 1 to 5 rating plus a short note per registration, shared or not by the participant), consented outcomes and the organiser report that shows them (ADR-047). The new report reads those records instead of keeping a second copy.

Genuinely missing: eligibility criteria and a check at the moment of applying; a gate on the number of places with a waiting list and promotion in order; versioned participation terms with acceptance recorded against the exact version; tasks owned by workspace member accounts and finished by that member; participant-visible tasks; in-app due-soon and overdue reminders; and fill rate, waiting list and acceptance figures.

## Decision

Add an optional additive module installed by `migrateCustomerProgrammeTerms`. It first installs the economics, coordination (with participants) and member-role modules, which bring in the organisation profile module, then one table family in `customer_private` and eleven restricted functions in `customer`.

- **Terms.** `programme_terms_revision` is an append-only version per programme: what is included, what is not, cancelling, changes, whether a fee applies and a change reason. A new version needs the version the organiser saw. Nothing is rewritten. Fees are never stored here: when terms say a fee applies, the participant view reads the organiser's own `PARTICIPANT_FEE` ledger entries for that registration as exact decimal strings per currency, and says nothing is collected.
- **Acceptance.** `programme_terms_acceptance` records the registration and the exact terms version, once. Applying names the latest version and records acceptance together with the place or the waiting spot. When a newer version exists the participant sees "accept the new terms"; the place is kept but the organiser report and a reminder show the missing acceptance.
- **Eligibility.** `programme_eligibility_revision` is an append-only version of allowed commercial roles, sectors, countries, company size bands and a minimum count of filled organisation profile details (of seven). Empty means anything. At apply time the database reads the head revision of the organisation profile of a workspace the applicant belongs to and checks it. The result is coded reasons only (`NO_PROFILE`, `ROLE`, `SECTOR`, `COUNTRY`, `SIZE`, `COMPLETENESS`). The participant sees a plain sentence for each code; the organiser sees which rule failed and never a profile value. A not-eligible answer is recorded as a revision and nothing is accepted; the participant may fix their profile and apply again, and the earlier answers stay in the history with the criteria version they were checked against.
- **Places.** A rule revision holds the minimum to run and whether there is a waiting list. The maximum is the existing capacity record: setting places inserts the next capacity revision through the same version order, so there is one maximum. Places are enforced in the database: every command takes a per-programme advisory lock, and a trigger on the enrolment history takes the same lock and refuses a `PLACED` row when live places already equal the maximum, so no path can overbook. Without a waiting list a full programme refuses the application and records nothing; with one the applicant gets the next queue number. A place is live only while its registration is active, so a withdrawal or removal through the existing participant module frees it. Giving a place up, and every apply, set-places and refresh command, move waiting people up in queue order, one event each. A freed place is filled at the next of those commands, or by the organiser's "Move waiting people up", which the report offers when places are free and people wait. The maximum cannot be set below the number of people already placed, and this holds for every writer: a trigger on the capacity record refuses a lower value, so the older capacity port (ADR-047) is unchanged except that it can no longer cut places below the people placed.
- **Task owners.** `programme_task_plan_revision` is an append-only owner and visibility per task. The owner must be a current member of the workspace (original member or active invited member with an enabled account; a revoked or disabled member is not in the directory). Members appear under an opaque key, never a principal id. The owner finishes only their own task: the function writes the next revision of the existing task record, so there is one task history, and the organiser's older copy goes stale through the existing version check. A plain member can finish a task given to them without being able to see the programme. Tasks marked visible are readable by participants who hold a place, through the participant authority, as title and due date only, and never once finished or cancelled.
- **Reminders.** `customer.my_programme_reminders(as_of)` is a read model for the signed-in person only: tasks given to them, visible programme tasks for places they hold, and terms awaiting acceptance. Due soon is today and the next three days, overdue is before today, the date defaults to today and the same date always gives the same answer. They appear in the page; no email, text or push message exists.
- **Report.** `customer.programme_places_report` computes, from this programme's own records: places taken against the maximum and the fill rate as an exact permille, minimum reached or not, waiting list length, the funnel from registered to placed, not eligible and gave up, not-eligible counts by reason, terms acceptance rate, satisfaction (read from the existing shared ratings) and shared outcomes, and a roster. Nothing is estimated, `verified` is false and each block says who reported it.
- **Authority.** Organiser functions use the existing programme management check (workspace owner or admin, programme in the workspace, executor available). Applying, accepting and giving up require the signed-in account to own an active registration. Finishing a task requires current workspace membership and being its owner. Authority always comes from the fresh provider proof; nothing takes a subject, role or organisation from the caller. A plain member, other workspace, other participant or revoked or disabled account reads as no access.
- **Retries and concurrency.** Every command carries an id. An exact retry returns the first answer, the same id with different content conflicts, and edits name the version they saw. Writers on one programme are serialised by locks taken in a fixed order (coordination, then places). Free text must begin with `Synthetic `.
- **Events.** One existing event, audit and outbox chain per real change (`customer.programme_terms.*`, or the existing coordination chain for a finished task), carrying identifiers and the kind only, never terms text, a reason code, a rating or an amount.
- **Interface.** Under the existing delegation pages: a "Terms, eligibility and places" panel per programme with Terms, Eligibility, Capacity and Reminders sections, "Your place" per registration on the participant page and "Your reminders" at the top of it. Primary actions are explicit, empty states are calm, and writes show a confirmation, a stale warning or an unconfirmed retry that keeps the same command id.

## Synthetic-only boundary

The route is off unless the workspace, programme, participant, economics and coordination flags and `CORRIDRA_CUSTOMER_PROGRAMME_TERMS_ENABLED` are set, the environment is synthetic and the deployment passes the explicit production opt-in in [ADR049](0049-synthetic-production-opt-in-and-open-admission.md). D01 to D06 and D08 stay closed. No money moves, no invoice or refund exists, nothing is sent and no file is stored.

## Blast radius and rollback

New objects only. Core tables, the earlier customer modules, staff identity, RLS and every existing function, ACL, policy and trigger are unchanged, and the installer is verified on a populated database to leave them identical. The deliberate cross-module touches are a new capacity revision when places are set, through the existing version order, and the guard trigger on the capacity record. Rollback is disabling the flag and redeploying the previous application; the additive schema and its history are retained and never dropped.

## Not included

Manual approval or override of an ineligible applicant, notices or messages to anyone (outreach is gated), email, text or push reminders, refunds, invoices or payment collection (terms only describe them), file attachments, eligibility based on verified facts (profiles are self-reported), automatic promotion when a registration ends outside this module before the next command, recurring or per-person reminder preferences, and ownership by non-members.

## Required acceptance

Populated additive upgrade and repeat install with an exact registry hash, direct SQL denial for runtime and auth, owner and admin writes against member, outsider, forged-workspace, revoked and disabled denial, expired proof, stale versions, exact retries and changed-content conflicts, immutable history, event content privacy, synthetic-only text, concurrent applications that never overbook and keep queue order, the database-level overbooking and transition guards, terms acceptance per version, coded eligibility reasons without profile content, fee read-through without money storage, task owners and finishing, participant-visible tasks, deterministic reminders, a mutation check of the authority gate, protocol browser tests with keyboard and accessibility at four widths, and the full original suites on the exact merged head.
