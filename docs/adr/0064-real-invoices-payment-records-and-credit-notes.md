# ADR-064: Real invoices, payment records and credit notes (billing stage 2)

Status: bounded implementation, default off, database control closed. Two switches must both be on before a real workspace can see an invoice or staff can issue one: the database control opened by the owner-run install job, and `CORRIDRA_CUSTOMER_REAL_INVOICING_ENABLED` (which also needs real plans on). No payment provider is involved and no money moves. Supplier details contain made-up fields, each marked "(madeup)", until the owner replaces them.

## Context

[ADR-062](0062-real-plans-subscriptions-and-entitlements.md) gave a real workspace a plan record and named stage 2 as real invoices with a legal number, tax lines and credit notes, payments recorded by hand. The contract keeps three rules that bound this stage: a billing processor is not a prerequisite for a lawful, manually invoiced managed service; an invoice is distinct from a confirmed payment; and built, tested, deployed, enabled and commercially live are different states.

The owner supplied the billing entity on 24 October 2026: Corridra Pte Ltd, 101 Robinson Road, Singapore 180239; a standard Singapore GST rule ("fine if not accurate"); a numbering format following ordinary Singapore practice for this kind of business; and a finance person named "Sadat FPA". Where a legal detail was missing (UEN, GST registration number, bank details, the finance person's staff account), the owner asked for made-up values with a mention, so each is stored with "(madeup)" in its text and listed in the supplier record as a placeholder field.

## Decision

New optional module `packages/db/customer-invoicing-real-migrations/0001_real_invoicing.sql`, registry `customer_invoicing_real_migrations.applied` (SHA-256, history-mismatch refusal, advisory lock), installed by `migrateCustomerInvoicingReal` after the billing, real billing and invoicing modules. It adds only new objects. The sample invoicing module is untouched and still serves sample workspaces only.

## Not included

A payment provider, card or bank collection, refunds, reminders and dunning, emailing or PDF generation of a document (the document is the data the screen prints), SGD conversion of foreign-currency GST, tax rules for other countries, discounts, proration, usage charges, invoicing anything but a plan period, and enabling for any workspace by default. Entitlement enforcement is still absent. Stage 3 (a payment provider) is a separate decision and ADR.

## Limits the owner should know

- The GST rule is a standard 9% rule applied exactly as described; it is not tax advice and has not been reviewed by an accountant. Check it, the registration status and the numbering with your finance adviser before any document leaves the building.
- Until real values replace the made-up fields, every document says it is not a valid tax invoice. Do not send such a document to a customer as if it were real.
- The finance person is a label until the owner supplies that person's staff account email in `set_supplier`. Without it, any second owner or admin can approve.
- With one staff person only, nothing can be approved unless the owner records `allow_single_approver`; that choice is recorded and shown on each document it covers.
- Real-user ease of the screens is unproven.

## Rollback

Pause the control with the install job (`real_invoicing_action` = pause) or unset the switch. Documents, payments and history stay and are readable to the database owner. Sample workspaces are unaffected either way.

## Screens and routes (stage 2b)

Customers read real invoices on the same billing page and through the same endpoint as plans: `GET /api/customer-billing?scope=invoices&workspaceId=...`. A real deployment (real use on) answers with the real shape from `customer.real_invoicing_overview`; a sample deployment keeps answering with sample invoices. Both switches (`CORRIDRA_CUSTOMER_REAL_BILLING_ENABLED`, `CORRIDRA_CUSTOMER_REAL_INVOICING_ENABLED`) and the database control must be on; otherwise the section is simply absent. The page shows each document as a tax invoice or plain invoice with supplier and bill-to blocks, the tax lines and the totals, the payments and credit notes the team recorded, a visible "(madeup)" notice naming the supplier fields that are not real yet, and a print button (print or save as PDF). Customers cannot act on an invoice. The browser bundle carries no server authority code.

Staff use `/api/workspace/customer-billing?invoicing=real` with the scopes `pending`, `invoices`, `invoice` and, for changes, a POST naming one of the actions `prepare_invoice`, `prepare_credit`, `decide` or `payment`. The separate query marker keeps real `payment` apart from the sample command of the same name. A closed control answers 503 with the same plain message.

## Required acceptance

`v3-real-invoicing-ui.spec.ts` (customer screen): a real tax invoice shows both parties, GST at its rate, payments and credit notes, the made-up notice and a print button; a plain invoice shows no GST registration line and no notice; members, empty and switched-off states; a malformed, sample-flagged, payment-taken or wrong-workspace answer is refused; no sample wording; axe clean.

`customer-invoicing-real.test.ts`: install is repeatable and refuses a changed history; the control starts closed and the supplier details are seeded with every made-up field marked; a closed control answers customers 55000 and staff 503; a sample operator and read-only staff roles are refused; prepare then second-person approval with GST in exact minor units, the legal number and the made-up label on the face; the preparer cannot approve; exact payments in order, never above outstanding, never future dated, stale position refused, references plain; credit notes in their own series, refused above the unpaid amount and at approval after the invoice changed; rejection frees the period; numbers gap free and unique under concurrent approval; the database refuses a gap, a repeat, a wrong total and any rewrite; the runtime logins reach no table or private function; command replay and conflict; events carry no amounts or text; rounding half up for inclusive and exclusive prices; a non-SGD plan and a tax-to-be-confirmed plan are refused while registered; an unregistered supplier issues a plain Invoice and old documents keep their version; single-approver and named-approver rules; pausing closes everyone and keeps every record. The sample invoicing, billing and real billing suites pass unchanged.
