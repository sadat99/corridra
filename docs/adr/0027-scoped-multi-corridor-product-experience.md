# ADR-027: Scoped multi-corridor product experience

Status: implemented for V2 Part 3; release evidence is recorded separately.

Public journeys start with a commercial job and registered company country/target market. BD–China, BD–Spain and BD–EU pages distinguish schema support, pilot status, actual availability and unavailable operations. EU is a trade bloc, not a jurisdiction decision. Intake uses Part 2 requirement fields and a versioned structured declaration, while preserving legacy briefs and explicitly refusing real customer admission. Short synthetic drafts remain session-local for 24 hours. Country/job selection cannot grant route readiness or tenant rights.

Neon Auth continues to verify customer identity. My missions redeems an explicit expiring CLIENT delivery capability after verified sign-in; matching email grants nothing. Capabilities stay in memory, use POST bodies and never become operator membership. Guest client/institutional links and operator password/TOTP remain intact. Controlled document collection remains separate from declarations; this part adds no unrestricted upload or raw evidence download.

Owners/admins with fresh MFA approve exact requirements, target assessment snapshots, quotation revisions and agenda rows for one canonical publication. Source retention, current publisher access, audience and capability validity are checked on every read. No private contacts, relationship owners or unrelated quotations are copied. Original supplier terms remain original; comparison preserves missing fields and rejects incompatible or stale scope. Translations do not replace original text. Registered currency precision and integer minor units remain authoritative; decimal entry converts exactly or rejects excess precision.

Purpose-limited buyer capabilities may receive one immutable supplier/distributor/partner disclosure. Suppliers may submit an explicitly enabled quotation declaration against the disclosed RFQ; recipients may express enabled meeting interest. These append-only, retained and audited declarations never become verified claims, accepted appointments, canonical quotations, orders or financial authority automatically. Existing origin/rate/expiry/revocation/opt-out and outreach-policy checks remain. No automatic email, WeChat or LinkedIn transport is added.

Delegation contexts and agenda versions reference existing programmes, independent participant missions and actual meetings. Confirmed shared activity requires matching actual schedule/venue and excludes duplicate attribution. Institutional links opt in explicitly, pin the approved context/agenda as of link creation and include only participant/meeting scope already selected in publications. Organisers never receive participant quotation extensions. Distinct companies use explicit public-source organisation attribution when available, otherwise an openly labelled declared-name/country basis. Shared held activity counts once; individual commercial engagement counts remain separate. Changed canonical appointments become review-required rather than invented bookings.

Migration 0025 adds append-only scoped extension tables, one false-by-default institutional opt-in column and guarded projection wrappers. Previous migrations remain immutable. Runtime cannot execute the old unwrapped report or private source-validation helpers. Restricted-role tests, actual source expiry, publisher revocation, populated-upgrade digests and historical export byte comparisons are required. Deployments do not migrate databases. Apply separately to the inspected live synthetic branch after isolated validation and final-head CI. Rollback preserves additive rows and audit history, restores a compatible Part 2 application, and disables new entry points; never reverse the migration destructively.

Reusable English and Bangla date/number presentation and useful Bangla intake guidance are implemented. Chinese originals render in web, CSV and embedded-font PDF. Full multilingual UI, translation service, persistent customer mission discovery, real customer mission activation, travel booking, supplier assurance and automatic outreach remain unavailable. Part 4 must consume explicit scoped tasks and independent contribution/reward approvals rather than treating a reply, invitation or agenda item as authority.
