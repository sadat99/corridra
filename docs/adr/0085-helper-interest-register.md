# ADR-085: The helper interest register

Status: Proposed, built behind its own switch that is off by default (`CORRIDRA_HELPER_INTEREST`) and behind the Network switch (`CORRIDRA_NETWORK_ENABLED`). The database module is tested on PostgreSQL 16 and is NOT installed anywhere; the owner installs it later through the existing installer (`field_modules_action = helper_interest`). Nothing is enabled. The privacy notice wording was written and approved on 15 November 2026 under the direction recorded below; turning the switch on is still a separate owner step. Core migration 0030 (rate limit key purge, decision 13) is applied by the next `apply=yes` install run, not by a merge.

Authority: owner decisions of 14 November 2026 at about 23:10Z, relayed by Orchestrator 13, answering "all recommended" to four questions: (1) a public form allowed only for this purpose, with on-screen text that it is not an invitation, a job or a reward; (2) submissions stored in Corridra, readable only by owner and admin (row security), one durable event per create and per delete, automatic deletion after 180 days and deletion on request; (3) the owner approves new privacy notice wording before go-live, and the form shows only while `CORRIDRA_NETWORK_ENABLED` is on; (4) acknowledgement on the screen only, no email. 15 November 2026, relayed second hand by Orchestrator 12 through Orchestrator 13 (the builder did not hear it first hand): the owner said "u update the privacy section to best extent and approve by yourself". Orchestrator 12 is the delegated approver of the privacy wording, and the owner also directed that the hashed email must be deleted, not kept. This supersedes decision (3) for the wording only. It is not approval to go live: both switches stay unset until the owner sets them. Earlier: 14 November, "contributor interest register ... FULL route, only after the privacy review". ADR-020 (identity separation), ADR-003 (tenancy and row security), ADR-005 (events), ADR-057 (invited helpers only) and ADR-080 (the closest precedent for a public form with limits) apply unchanged.

## Context

Corridra Network work is by invitation only (ADR-057): a staff owner or admin prepares a reviewed invitation for a person they already know. The helper door (`/access/team`) therefore had nothing for someone who is not invited yet and would like to be. This ADR adds one small, bounded thing: a note of interest. It is not an application, not an invitation, not a job and not a reward, it does not create an account, session or membership, it sends nothing and it opens nothing in the Network. Real outreach, screening, contributor payments and the Network gate stay closed and are not touched.

## Decision

1. **One public form, one purpose.** On `/access/team`, inside a disclosure headed "Not invited yet?", two short steps ask for a name, an email address, a country and a short reason in the person's own words (10 to 500 characters, one plain line). The text says it is not an invitation, a job or a reward. The reason is plain text: it is stored as written, shown as text and never interpreted as HTML.
2. **Two switches, both exactly "true".** The form, the privacy notice section and the public route exist only while `CORRIDRA_HELPER_INTEREST` and `CORRIDRA_NETWORK_ENABLED` are both "true" and the module is installed and open. Otherwise the page is what it was and the public route answers 404 as if it did not exist. Both are described in `lib/owner-switches.ts` and `lib/ux-switches.ts`.
3. **Anchor.** A visitor has no workspace and no actor, but every event needs both (`app.domain_event.tenant_id` and `actor_id` are not null, ADR-005). The register is anchored to the Corridra staff workspace that already receives real intake, `customer_private.real_use_configuration.intake_tenant_id`, and the events name the service principal that configuration already records as `executor_id` (the same executor the installer pins for customer events). It is open only while real admission is open and that workspace is a real one; if the owner pauses real admission the register closes and nothing is stored (retention keeps running). No sample workspace ever holds a real person's entry.
4. **What is stored.** Name, email (trimmed, lower case), country (two letters), reason, a creation time and an id. No IP address, no user agent, no session, no cookie. No client address is read, hashed or kept. The rate limit budget has two keys only, a keyed one way hash (HMAC with the server secret) of the email address and one global key; no raw value is kept anywhere. The hashed email key is used only to limit repeat sign-ups. It cannot be turned back into the address, and it is removed by the rate limit purge (decision 13) once it is more than two hours old; the privacy notice says so.
5. **Who can read.** Only an owner or admin of the anchor workspace, with a fresh authenticator check, through a row security policy on `app.helper_interest`. A member of any other role, a helper, a signed out visitor and an owner of another workspace read nothing. Nothing is granted to write the table: it is created through `app.helper_interest_submit`, deleted through `app.helper_interest_remove` or the retention purge, and a trigger rejects any update.
6. **No enumeration, no duplicates.** The email is unique within the workspace after normalisation. A repeat of an address already registered changes nothing and returns exactly what a first sign-up returns (HTTP 202 and the same body), so the answer says nothing about who signed up before. A filled hidden field (the honeypot) stores nothing and returns the same answer. Bad input is refused with 400 and stores nothing.
7. **Limits.** A body of at most 4 KB; per email address 6 sign-ups an hour and 60 an hour in all (there is no per-client limit, because no client address is used), using the existing `app.intake_budget` (429 past a limit); at most 5,000 entries in the register (54000, answered 429). The route checks the origin and the JSON content type.
8. **Retention: 180 days, with no job.** A row older than 180 days is invisible at once (the row security policy and every read test the age) and is physically deleted, with one delete event per row, whenever someone submits and whenever an owner or admin opens the list (`app.helper_interest_purge`, 200 rows a call). There is no scheduler and no new secret. A returning visitor whose own entry has expired is handled at sign-up even when more than 200 expired rows are waiting: that one entry is removed first, with its retention event, so it can never suppress the new entry. Deletion on request: an owner or admin deletes an entry from the list (confirm step, fresh authenticator check); the person asks through the privacy contact already named in the privacy notice.
9. **Events carry ids only.** One `helper_interest.created` (version 1) per create and one `helper_interest.deleted` (version 2) per delete, with the audit entry and outbox rows in the same transaction. The payload is `{id, initiator_kind}` (and `reason` of `request` or `retention` on a delete). Never a name, an email address or a word of the reason. Staff deletion records the staff principal as actor; create and retention name the executor.
10. **Operator surface.** One panel in the existing Network area, for owners and admins only, that lists the entries (name, email, country, date, their words) and deletes one. No edit, no export, no message, no count of anything else. The panel does not exist while the module is not installed, and it works while the form is switched off so that an entry can always be deleted.
11. **Install.** A new optional module `helper_interest` in `packages/db/helper-interest-migrations/`, applied by `migrateHelperInterest` (advisory lock, hash guarded and forward only) through `field_modules_action = helper_interest` in the existing workflow. It installs no other module and refuses, naming the step, unless the real-use modules are installed. Installing changes nothing anyone can see. The owner approves the protected environment as for every install; no production SQL is run.
12. **Privacy notice.** A section "Telling us you want to be a field helper" is added to `/privacy`, with its contents entry, only while both switches are on. The wording was written on 15 November 2026 and approved by Orchestrator 12 as the delegated approver (see Authority). It says what is collected (name, email, country, reason) and why (only so the Corridra team can get in touch with people interested in helping the network), who can read it (owners and administrators of the staff workspace that receives requests), that it is kept 180 days and deleted on request (by writing to the contact already named in the notice), that a scrambled one-way form of the email is used only to limit repeat sign-ups and is removed after about two hours, that nothing is used for marketing, sold or shared for others' purposes, that the form sets no cookies, and that it shows only while the Network is on. The switches stay unset until the owner sets them.
13. **Rate limit keys do not outlive their use.** `app.intake_budget` (0007) and `customer.real_request_budget` (real-use 0004) count calls per keyed hash per hour in `app.intake_rate_limit` and never removed a row, so the helper email hash, and the keys of mission intake (sample and real), buyer replies and delivery, stayed forever. A row more than two hours old has no effect (its hour ended over an hour ago and the next call with that key resets its count to 1), so deleting it changes no answer. Core migration `packages/db/migrations/0030_intake_rate_limit_cleanup.sql` adds ONE function, `app.intake_rate_limit_purge()` (security definer, fixed search path, executable by the runtime login only), that deletes up to 500 rows with `window_start` older than two hours, picked `FOR UPDATE SKIP LOCKED` so it never waits. It also purges the rows already that old once and adds an index on `window_start`. It replaces no function, so both writers are covered with no module install. **The purge is never called inside a budget function or transaction.** An earlier draft cleaned inside `app.intake_budget`; independent review showed that a delete of other callers' rows inside a budget transaction can deadlock (40P01) when two calls wait on each other's rows, including when one request spends two budgets (a cleaning call and a counting call hold and want each other's keys). Each public form therefore runs the purge as its own statement, in its own transaction, after its budget query (`purgeIntakeRateLimit` in `@corridra/db`: mission intake in both modes, buyer replies, delivery, and both helper interest budget statements). Any error is ignored, so no answer can change and nothing breaks before 0030 is installed. A key leaves the table at the first form use after it is more than two hours old, and within the next few uses if more than 500 are waiting. The two hour threshold never touches a current or previous hour row, whatever the time zone or the hour boundary. The purge runs only when a form is used, so on a quiet site a stale key waits for the next use, and the notice says "the next time one of our public forms is used after that". No event is written (the counter is not a domain record), and no evidence, money or identity row is touched.

## Blast radius

- New: one table `app.helper_interest` (row security on, one read policy, one update guard trigger) and eight functions in `packages/db/helper-interest-migrations/0001_helper_interest_register.sql`: two limits (retention, cap), two private ones (`helper_interest_anchor`, `helper_interest_event`) that no login can call, and `open`, `purge`, `submit`, `remove` plus the retention limit, which the runtime role may call. One service (`packages/auth/src/helper-interest.ts`), one route, one form, one operator panel, one privacy section.
- Existing objects: no table, policy, constraint, grant, trigger or function is changed or replaced. Two permission names (`helper.interest.read`, `helper.interest.remove`, owner and admin, fresh authenticator check) are added to the permission registry in `packages/domain` and `packages/db`; no existing permission changes. Core migration 0030 adds one function (`app.intake_rate_limit_purge`) and one index on `app.intake_rate_limit` (decision 13); it replaces no function, and the four public forms call the purge after their budget query. The table stays revoked from the runtime and auth logins. `helper-interest-migrations/0001` is not touched by it.
- Not touched: customer, staff and contributor identity separation, MFA, the Network gate, invitations, outreach, email, payments, AI, the ledger and the work log.

## Not decided here

- Retention of the events themselves follows ADR-005 (immutable ids only). No legal review of the notice wording is recorded.
- Any follow-up from an entry (an invitation is a separate, existing, reviewed step). A signed-in reconcile with `lib/front-doors.ts`, and languages other than English (native review).
