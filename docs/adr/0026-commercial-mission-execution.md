# ADR-026: Versioned commercial mission execution

Status: implemented locally; release acceptance pending.

Corridra V2 Part 2 adds requirement submissions, typed counterparty assessments, RFQs, original quotations and revisions, human decisions and trip schedules. These records use canonical missions, target accounts, relationships, claims and sources. They do not create separate country applications or replacement state machines. Original intake, CommercialIntent, target-profile and research assessments remain authoritative source records; structured requirements supplement their histories.

Requirement and assessment definitions acquire immutable JSON schemas. Existing definitions receive schemaVersion 0, meaning that no Part 2 schema was declared, rather than a guessed interpretation. Explicit owner installation creates schemaVersion 1 definitions for the reusable mission catalogue. Packs must pin the exact definitions. Requirements also pin a mission assignment, definition, schema version, original declaration, source/claim and author, with an optional personally recorded privileged reviewer. Reclassification and changed requirements invalidate new decisions against stale versions without deleting old submissions.

Each assessment retains typed criteria, assessed requirements, declared basis, method/version, assessor, date, unknowns, gaps and rationale. Directory presence and an operator FIT result confer no verified-fact, engineering-certification, regulatory-approval or independent-audit authority. Existing research and evidence verification paths keep their separate permissions and states.

RFQs preserve the mission/buyer context, exact requirements, intended target accounts, source and claim. Quotations must belong to a requested supplier target. Revisions preserve RFQ, supplier and requirement parentage and append to the immediately previous revision. Original currency, exact integer minor-unit strings and original terms remain separate from calculations and customer financial outcomes. Terms omitted by a supplier remain omitted. Comparison refuses missing scope, expired validity, differing specifications/quantities/units/currencies/Incoterms/shipping, contradictory supplied totals and unavailable or stale evidence. Unit price times integer quantity is explicitly a derived subtotal, with recorded inputs and calculation time; tooling, setup, packaging and transport remain separate. No FX rate is invented.

Meetings gain a typed activity kind and optional target origin. An introduction-backed meeting keeps its original parent; a supplier discussion or site visit can instead reference an accessible target. Exactly one parent is required. The existing proposal, acceptance, scheduling, attendance, no-show, cancellation, rescheduling and owned-follow-up contracts apply to both. The original SQL guards remain, with only parent validation extended. Accepted and held visits do not imply commercial success. Supplier-origin opportunities still use Part 1's commercial-origin contract rather than inferring an introduction or held meeting.

Trip schedule versions contain commercial locations, events, candidates, operator availability, constraints, logistics references and confirmed meeting references. A confirmed appointment resolves its current location/time/status from the meeting; it cannot supply independent fabricated appointment details. No live journey-time estimation, venue inventory or tourism booking exists.

New records are tenant scoped, append only, emit ordinary domain events/outbox records and enforce authorization again in SQL. Target-private assessments, offers and decisions follow existing relationship visibility. RFQs are visible only when all requested targets are accessible. Commands bind idempotency to normalized input and actor. Existing financial transitions, accounting confirmation, compensation and reproducible outcome projections are unchanged.

The operator workspace adds progressive forms and original-offer history. It reuses existing source/claim collection, counterparty research, meeting transitions and commercial follow-through. Route, job, owner, stage and overdue filters execute on the server before pagination. Configuration administration and customer activation remain separate from mission execution.

Migration 0023 creates the seven append-only execution/command tables and adds definition schemas. Migration 0024 adds the meeting context and extends its parent access/guard. Earlier migrations and original supplied documents remain immutable. Disable new entry points and retain additive tables/history for rollback. Customer-data, outreach, scanner, legal/pilot and independent-assurance gates remain in the deferred controls register.
