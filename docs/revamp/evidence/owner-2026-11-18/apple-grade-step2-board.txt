Corridra · Step two of two · Look and feel · Proposal
Same brand. One job per screen. The sentence that matters, bigger.
Step one changed what the product does, and it is shipped. Step two changes how it feels in the hand: on a phone in sunlight, in a second language, with one thumb, on a slow network. We keep every colour, font, trust mark and holder shape. We change size, space, order and what sits on screen at once.
11 screens, 3 waves, before beside after, nothing shipped.

PRINCIPLES
01 One job per screen. Each view asks one question and offers one cobalt action. Everything else waits behind a named disclosure. Evidence: /access/customer is 2,713 px (3.2 phone screens) and shows 7 buttons for a one-choice door.
02 The key sentence bigger, the chrome quieter. The move, the step and the next action get the type size. Mono labels, breadcrumbs and callout boxes step back. Evidence: the Today move and "Stage 3 of 6" are 14 px today; on /plan, three framed boxes sit above the first question.
03 Fewer boxes, more air. Lines and spacing group things. A card appears only when it is a real object: a quote, a next step, a supplier. Evidence: /plan nests a box, a box, a button and a card before the first choice at about 760 px.
04 A fixed bottom action area on phone. The one action and its reassurance line stay under the thumb. It never covers content and it never becomes a tab bar. Evidence: Continue on /plan sits about 1,450 px down, under five choices and a select.
05 Listen and language one tap away, icon plus word. Listen, the language and Menu sit in the top bar of every screen, each with a visible word. No icon stands alone. Evidence: on a phone, Listen lives inside the "?" popover today, two taps away, and "?" has no word.
(06 Motion is a sixth principle, shown in its own strip: it must never carry meaning.)

WHAT WE DELIBERATELY KEEP: AC/1 palette, cobalt is the only action fill; navy chrome; copper as ornament only; Newsreader and Geist, Geist Mono labels; the five trust labels, exactly; hatched Not stated, striped Excluded; holder shapes circle, square, diamond, half-disc; the guided standard and its eight rules; 44 px targets, AA contrast; drawer navigation, no bottom tab bar; light only. The look changes inside these tokens. It does not repaint the brand.
Design note: the five moves were chosen from the recon (page heights from the live capture, type sizes from the signed-in screens notes, where the action sits in each screenshot), not from taste.

D MOTION AND FEEL: motion that explains, never decorates. Four behaviours, each under 300 ms (the Done tick 420 ms), product ease (0.2, 0.7, 0.2, 1). With reduced motion every one shows its still end state; none is needed to understand the screen. (a) The Done tick draws in once in 420 ms while the word changes Now to Done at the same moment. (b) The next-step card settles: rises 10 px and fades in over 260 ms, once. (c) The bottom action wakes up: when a choice is made the button fills cobalt and its label changes to "Continue"; before that it says why it waits ("Pick one to continue"). (d) Listen shows what it is reading: button word turns to "Pause", three bars move, line "Reading: line 2 of 5". Banned: parallax, auto-playing carousels, skeleton shimmer longer than one cycle, spring bounces, any motion that starts on its own. The live home Example carousel autoplays; it should keep a visible Pause.

E ROLLOUT: each wave ships behind the existing switches, passes every release check, and is verified on the live site before the next one starts.
Wave 1 Public and shell: screens 10 Shell (medium), 03 Access and Sign in (small), 01 Plan (medium), 02 Routes (small). All Class B. Owner decides: Listen and language in the top bar on every page; Pocket plus gains "Quick"; drop the Routes tabs and the Plan callout box; the six-row order of destinations. Risk: header crowding at 320 px and in Arabic; mitigation: orb only on phones, test at 320.
Wave 2 Signed-in core: 04 Your Move (medium), 05 Room (medium), 07 Road and lanes (small), 06 Compare (large, Class A). One Class A review: money and trust labels. Owner decides (OPEN): "Differences only" on by default; two-column compare on phones; white button inside navy cards everywhere; shortcut tiles move to Quick. Risk: exact money and Not stated must survive the new layout; mitigation: the existing money and hatching tests plus a Bangla and Chinese snapshot.
Wave 3 Outside people and the team: 08 Buyer reply (medium, Class A), 09 Before Send (small, Class A), 11 Operator desk (large). Two Class A reviews: consent and access display. Owner decides (OPEN): consent moves to step 2 next to Send; native review of BN, ZH, ES, AR, FR, DE copy; operator sidebar groups. Risk: changing where consent sits; mitigation: same words, same record, contract review before merge.
Do not do: Do not repaint the brand (no new colours, no new fonts, no gradient; cobalt stays the only action fill). Do not add a bottom tab bar (the drawer and Quick cover navigation; a tab bar would break the five-choice rule and fight the bottom action area). Do not add dark mode in step two. Do not convert money or add a sixth trust label to make a layout tidier. Do not add motion that plays on its own.
Dark mode: not in step two. The product is light only today and should stay light only through all three waves. Field reason: most users are on phones outdoors, dark interfaces lose contrast in direct sunlight. Contract reason: hatched Not stated, striped Excluded, trust label colours and status surfaces are checked for AA on light only; dark mode doubles that test surface in the waves that touch money. Shared phones: one setting would flip the look for every person using the phone. Instead: keep forced-colours support, add "Larger text" to the accessibility menu; revisit dark mode after Wave 3 with its own token set and a contrast test for the hatch patterns.
Sizes: small = mostly CSS and copy in one component; medium = a layout change across one page; large = several components and new tests; rough, set before any builder estimate.
