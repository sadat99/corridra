    What "comparable" means here.

    1Same delivery point

    A stated capacity is Self-declared until there is evidence or a defined check. The label travels with the number.

    Straight answers

    Questions people ask before they start.

    Who is Corridra?

    A cross-border market access service. We run scoped missions for companies in Bangladesh that buy from China or sell to the EU, the UK, the US, Australia and key Asian markets: we find the companies that matter, check what they claim, compare offers and set up meetings and visits.

    For any other route, we say plainly whether we can help.

    Which markets do you work in?

    From Bangladesh, we run missions to China for buying, and to the EU, the UK, the US, Australia and key Asian markets for selling.

    • Open now: those routes, from Bangladesh.
    • Selective: the reverse directions, into Bangladesh, taken case by case.
    • Any other route: ask us. We tell you plainly if we can help.

    The page has two big doors because buying and selling are opposite problems: too many suppliers to compare, or too few buyers worth meeting.

    What does it cost?

    Missions are paid and scoped to what you need. After a short conversation we send you a quote for that mission. We do not publish prices, because checking two suppliers and comparing a full machinery purchase are different pieces of work.

    You see the quote and decide before any work starts.

    Will you replace my agent, my chamber or my fair?

    No. Keep the people you trust. Corridra works alongside your agent, your suppliers, chambers and fairs, and fills the gaps: who else is worth meeting, what an offer leaves out, and what happened after each meeting.

    Do you work with chambers and trade bodies?

    We want to, and the delegation view is built for them. We do not name any organisation as a partner unless it has agreed, and none is named on this page.

    Is my data safe?

    These are the rules the product is built to:

    • Your mission is visible only to the people you allow.
    • Every access is checked on our server, with the least access needed.
    • Sensitive actions need a person to approve them.
    • Shared links are limited and expire.
    • Logs avoid personal details.

    These are principles we hold, not a certification. We will not claim one we do not have.

    Do you arrange visas or hotels?

    No. We do not arrange visas, hotels or tourism, and we cannot promise any visa outcome. We work on who to meet, what to compare, and what happens next.

    What happens after I send a request?

    A person reads your brief and contacts you with a few questions. After a short conversation you get a scoped quote. Nothing starts until you agree to it, and you can stop at any point before that.
