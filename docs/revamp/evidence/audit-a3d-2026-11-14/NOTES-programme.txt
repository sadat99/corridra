How they were taken
  The real CustomerProgrammes room (room on, service requests on, every other module off), its real stylesheets
  (collected by the bundler from the imports, plus globals and shells), inside WorkspaceShell with the customer frame; the
  page frame is drawn around it for "after" only, as the real page does. The programme API is answered by the test (sample
  data, no sign-in, no server). Same fixture, same data, same Chromium before and after, details closed.

Decision recorded: no helper line and no chips on these pages
  A 14 px helper line (the old closing sentence about what programme administration never grants) made the page longer
  than before: +15 px at 1440 and +40 px at 768 on the New tab, +26 px at 390, because the sentence next to Reload is
  already on one row at those widths and moving it up saves nothing. The status words "Planning record only · Sample data"
  stay where they were, under the programme name. So the frame holds the back link only; the sentence and the words are
  unchanged. A helper or chips would need a page that has height to spare.

Not measured
