Audit A3a (UI/UX.v3 audit, screens batch A): Home empty state, Requests and Workspaces, All Tools finder.
Taken 14 Nov 2026 in the cloud container.

How measured
- Real stylesheets: production build (pnpm build, next start), signed in as seeded QA test accounts (scripts/qa/signed-in-sweep.ts --serve,
  local PostgreSQL 16), Chromium. Disclosures closed except the phone fold of All Tools, which is open by default from 640 px (default state).
- Screens = full document height (header, content, footer) divided by the viewport height: 390x844, 768x1024, 1440x900.
  A value of 1.00 means the content is shorter than one window and the footer sits at the bottom of the window.
- "home-empty" is a signed-in customer with no workspace and no work (a fifth QA account made for the measure only).
  "home-filled", "requests", "workspaces" and "all-tools" are the seeded customer (one workspace, 3 work rows).
- before = origin/main d13843e (PR 499 merged); after = this branch merged with main 9ba6ba8 (PR 500 merged).

Files
- before-<view>-<width>.png and after-<view>-<width>.png, full page. before-lengths.json and after-lengths.json hold the numbers.
