What the pictures show (all at 390, 768 and 1440 px wide):
- profile-first-profile-*, profile-existing-summary-*, profile-existing-edit-*: before (the single form on main) and after (three steps, first step shown).

page-frame.css, page-budget.css, profile-steps.css) in the real customer frame (main.ux-customer, section as a direct
child), production esbuild bundle, profile API answered by the script. Details closed. Height is the height of main
divided by the viewport height (844 high at 390, 1024 at 768, 900 at 1440). The fixture has no left bar and no footer, so a
real page is a little longer (the footer is about 60 px); a signed-in page cannot be drawn without a session here.
