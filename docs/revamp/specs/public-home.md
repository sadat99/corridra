## Then: changes allowed in Wave 3

1. **Fast first step.** The first screen of `/plan` already asks only the job (when the link has not set it), which is the minimum useful first action. Keep it that way: no field moves between steps, so every element id, name and test hook stays where it is. (Decision 4 Nov 2026, after S3's audit: moving the target market onto step 1 would gain nothing and move a hook used by about 12 tests.)
2. **One primary action per section** on the home and each public page (UX gate rule). Secondary actions are quieter.
3. **Honest proof only.** No "Trusted by", "Results", counts, logos or testimonials. Sample material says "sample".
4. **English only.** Keep the language chip and the existing Bangla and Chinese code; add no new translated strings and claim no reviewed translation.
5. **Signed-in footer fix**, if a test shows the public footer offering sign-in to a signed-in person: change only the footer's own wording or link choice.

## Owner decision, 6 November 2026: home page concept G

The owner asked for a landing revamp of 2 to 2.5 desktop screens (verbatim: "landing could be 2-2.5x screen size ij desktop"), reviewed six concepts (A to F) and chose, verbatim: "E with F". Concept G is the result. Mockup and screenshots: [evidence/landing-g](../../agents/evidence/landing-g/) (`concepts.html?v=g`, 1440 and 390).

- **Hero (E).** Eyebrow, h1 "Make market entry deliberate." (unchanged), lead "Watch one request become a decision. Then start yours.", the three job rows with the same `/plan?job=buyers|sourcing|delegations` links and accessible names, "Something else? Tell us your business goal" to `/plan`, the two calm lines. Beside them the Mission Player.
- **Mission Player.** An `ol` named "How a mission works" with the six steps and their existing copy (descriptions stay in the accessibility tree). Each step is a screen of sample data: Define (the request), Find (the existing four-row shortlist), Compare (the existing quote table), Visit, Decide, Follow through. A Selling and Sourcing example switch (tablist, Arrow, Home and End). It advances every 4 seconds with a visible Pause (WCAG 2.2.2), stops on hover and focus, never advances under reduced motion, and has Previous and Next buttons. Every sample says Example and Not real companies or suppliers.
- **Evidence (F).** Hook line "Know who is real before you fly." above "Every fact says where it comes from.", the four labels as a numbered list, How We Label Facts to `/trust`, and an example dossier card that shows all four labels on its fields.
- **Routes (F).** Boarding-pass cards: Bangladesh to China, Bangladesh to Spain and Explore Every Route, same three links.
- **Closing band.** "Your request can look like this." with Start a Request. Header, footer and dock unchanged.
- **Budget.** 1440 × 900: 2.0 to 2.5 screens (owner). 390 × 844: at most 3.5 screens (owner, 6 November 2026, verbatim: "3.5 screens (Recommended)"). The mockup measures 2.33 and 3.50. No sideways scroll, 44 px targets, axe clean at 320, 390, 768 and 1440.
- **Budget with the Ledger footer.** Owner, 6 November 2026, verbatim answer: "Raise home to 3.0 / 4.25 (Recommended)". With the full footer the home may be up to 3.0 screens at 1440 × 900 and 4.25 at 390 × 844; measured 2.82 and 4.06.
- **Tests.** P-01 to P-07 stay unchanged. The AC1-03 pins (example tabs, four buyer rows, five quote rows, three route links, the `#mission-example` redirect) move to the player; the PR lists each changed assertion as owner-approved. New tests cover the player keyboard, Pause, reduced motion, the example switch and every job link.
- **Owner.** S3, after the PR 322 fixes. Not sensitive unless it touches the `/plan` submit path, consent, cookies or storage.

## Owner decision, 17 November 2026: home page Two Routes (replaces concept G)

## Owner decision, 18 November 2026: the board 3c in full (LP3)

Owner words, first hand to Guardian 29: "why didnt we implement https://claude.ai/artifact/END7KDT2Y3KhZZoYb8pExX with all the provided feedbacks and updates here? the page size limit is not applicable here. what is already done doesnt reflect this 100% corrcetly, only footer was said to remain as it is." The board is kept verbatim at [two-routes-board-3c.html](../evidence/owner-2026-11-18/two-routes-board-3c.html); its annotation strip, notes dock and design notes are not product.

- **Page.** Every product section of the board is on the home, in board order: the hero (brand line, a headline that becomes the chosen route's proposition, four doors with flags beside country names), the route bar (All Routes and the four routes, with a visible "Showing:" line that names the open route; review round 2 replaced the LP2 screen-reader-only line), the how-it-works strip until a door is chosen (closing with Open the China Route and Open Sell Abroad cards and a line of quiet choices that open a route in place), the China Route, Sell Abroad and Delegation with their worked examples open under their own headings, Something else, the mission line, the brief, trust with the example history, the eight people (preselected by the door), the questions, the route statuses with "Matches your route" and what Corridra is not, and the close that follows the chosen route. A phone start bar (one action and its reassurance line) appears once the hero has scrolled away, gives the page room so it never covers content, and hides at the page's own actions and the footer. /how-it-works and /trust keep what they had.
- **Budget.** The home has no page-length budget (owner words above). Every other page keeps its own. The page-length report still measures the home.
- **Brief.** Three answers build a draft card that belongs to the visitor. Its one action, Start a Request, opens `/plan?job=<key>&note=<words>`: the goal picks a job the form already accepts (sourcing, machinery_procurement, buyers, trade_fairs, delegations, or none) and the words fill the notes field of a fresh draft only. Nothing is sent from the home; there is no Send button.
- **Talk and WhatsApp.** Drawn only when `CORRIDRA_SUPPORT_WHATSAPP_NUMBER` is set, as a link to the same wa.me address the Help menus use; otherwise absent. The board's call-back and video-call buttons have no backend and are not built. The proof-page demo link is How We Label Facts to `/trust`.
- **Header.** Unchanged in LP3: the header for every public page, the home included, is built once by Wave 1 unit W1-A (Guardian 29, 18 November 2026, from the owner's step 2 board). The footer is unchanged.
- **Languages.** New words are English in every catalog; the board's Bangla, Chinese and Spanish hero lines are drafts shown only while that language's page words are released, with "Draft translation. Native review pending."
- **Shared patterns.** The new pieces are shared components in `apps/web/components/ux/`: `flag.tsx` (Flag, Flagged, RegionMark, the compass mark for Key Asian Markets, and RouteLine), `door-card.tsx`, `choice-chip.tsx`, `number-field.tsx`, `example-tag.tsx` (ExampleTag, Eyebrow), `start-bar.tsx` and `open-card.tsx` (OpenCard, QuietButton: in-page choices that change what the page shows, never a cobalt fill).
- **Review round 2 (Guardian 29).** Doors show a radio circle; the hero adds "Choose one. The page below opens that route." and "Each door opens its own worked example further down this page."; the mission steps are one connected rail (solid, dashed into and out of optional steps) that scrolls inside its own region on a phone; what each person gets sits on the selected surface with a selected edge; section titles stay at the UI/UX.v3 section-title size (`--text-h2`, 24 and 22 on a phone), sub-heads step down to 18 (`--text-lg`) and section leads are 18 (`--text-lg`); route test lines start with a small action dot. People, its tabs and the mission rail are shared with /how-it-works, so the selected tab there is cobalt too. A link to `/plan` that carries notes and meets a saved draft always asks; the question names a job only when the link chose it.
- **Where UI/UX.v3 wins over the board.** Colours, type and radii come from the tokens (no board hex, weights 400 to 600, Newsreader headings, rounding B, capsules for badges only); cobalt is the only action fill; no dark mode; reduced motion removes every slide and fade; the Start a Request action stays above the doors so it is on a phone's first screen.
