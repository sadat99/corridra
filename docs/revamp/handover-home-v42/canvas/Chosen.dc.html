Straight answers

Questions people ask before they start.

What does it cost?

Missions are paid and planned around what you need. After a short conversation we send you a quote for that mission. You see the quote and decide before any work starts.

What happens after I send a request?

A person reads your request and contacts you with a few questions. After a short conversation you get a quote for that work. Nothing starts until you agree to it.

Will you replace my agent, my chamber or my fair?

No. Keep the people you trust. Corridra works alongside your agent, your suppliers, chambers and fairs, and fills the gaps.

Is my data safe?

Your mission is visible only to the people you allow. Every access is checked on our server. Sensitive actions need a person to approve them. Shared links are limited and expire.

Plain limits

What Corridra is not.

  • Not a directory.A list of names is not access.
  • Not a marketplace.We are not trying to replace one.
  • Not a CRM or an ERP.We feed your tools, we do not replace them.
  • Not a travel agency or visa service.Tourism and visas are not our work.
  • Not a mass-outreach engine.No unsolicited messages on your behalf.
  • Not an autonomous negotiator.People decide, always.
  • Not a payment processor or lender.We record money. We do not move it.
  • Not a social network.Introductions have a purpose and clear limits.
