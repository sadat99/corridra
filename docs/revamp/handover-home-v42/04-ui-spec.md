## 0. Blockers and flags
- B1 (OWNER DECISION). The demand line "[BD] Bangladesh sells to [EU] EU [GB] UK [US] US [AU] Australia" needs five 22 px flags plus the verb and four names: about 440 px at 14 px text, about 370 px even at 12 px with tight gaps. The card inner width is about 264 px at 320 and about 334 px at 390; the 14 px line only fits from about 520 px viewport (builder must measure in a production build). Supply "[BD] Bangladesh buys from [CN] China" is about 250 px and fits one line at 320 only if the line gets the full card width (section 3). Proposed fallback (least deviation): below the width where the line fits, the demand line breaks at ONE fixed point: row 1 "[BD] Bangladesh sells to", row 2 "[EU] EU [GB] UK [US] US [AU] Australia" (about 264 px, fits at 320). No flag-and-name unit splits; "Australia" is never alone. From the fit width up (container query on the card, expected about 32 rem, measured) it is one line. The owner said "needs to be one line" at every width, so this needs the owner's decision. Other options are worse: flags below 22 px break L1; dropping the Bangladesh flag breaks L1; horizontal scroll breaks no-sideways-scroll; abbreviating Australia is new wording. Acceptance check 3 cannot pass literally at 320 and 390 for the demand card.

Use catalog keys that are whole templates with {from} and {to} slots (`home.v42.line.buys` = "{from} buys from {to}", `home.v42.line.sells` = "{from} sells to {to}"), not a fixed word prop. (Supersedes the architect's three-key word plan; the line composition changes slightly.)
- B3. Drop `home.tr.hint` (no longer true with the reveal directly under the choice block). `home.tr.q.sub` stays true.
- Flag: the live closing band repeats /plan?job= links that HomeBrief (#start) also holds; that is the recorded "/" exception under No repeated links (D9). Add no more repeats.
