BEFORE YOU START

Straight answers.

Who is Corridra?

Corridra runs commercial missions. We help find and qualify counterparties, compare offers, arrange business meetings and visits, and track the agreed next steps.

Which markets do you work in?

We accept sourcing requests from Bangladesh to China and selling requests from Bangladesh to the EU, UK, US, Australia and key Asian markets. Requests into Bangladesh are assessed case by case. For any other route, tell us what you need.

What does it cost?

Missions are paid and quoted based on the scope. After a short conversation, you can review the quote and decide whether to proceed.

Can I work with my existing agent or trade fair?

Yes. Corridra works alongside the people and events you already use. We can look beyond a fair list, check terms an offer leaves unclear, and track the follow-up.

Can a chamber or trade body organise a programme?

The programme tools are designed for organisers. Each company brings its own goals, and an organiser works with the programme information shared with them.

Who can see my mission?

Corridra limits private records to people with access to the relevant work. Shared links have limited access and can expire. Read the privacy notice.

Do you arrange visas or hotels?

Corridra’s scope is commercial work: counterparties, comparisons, meetings, factory visits and follow-up. Visas, hotels and tourism are outside that scope.

What happens after I send a request?

A person reviews your request, asks what they need to know and sends a quote for the agreed scope. You decide whether work goes ahead.
