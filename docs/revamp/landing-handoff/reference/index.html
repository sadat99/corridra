BEFORE YOU START

Common questions.

Who is Corridra?

Corridra is a cross-border business service. Our team helps you find and assess companies, compare offers, organise meetings and visits, and track the next steps.

Which markets do you work in?

The main sourcing route is Bangladesh to China. For sales, you can ask about the EU, UK, US, Australia and specific Asian markets. We assess inbound work and other routes individually.

What does it cost?

Corridra quotes for the agreed work. You can review the scope and price before deciding whether to proceed.

Can I work with my existing agent or trade fair?

Yes. Share the contacts or meetings you already have. Corridra can help check fit, find additional companies and book meetings around your existing schedule.

Can a chamber or trade body organise a programme?

Yes. Share the group’s dates, focus and expected participants. Each company can have its own goals, and organisers work from the programme information shared with them.

Who can see my mission?

Corridra limits private records to people with access to the relevant work. Shared links have limited access and can expire. Read the privacy notice.

Do you arrange visas or hotels?

Your team handles visas, hotels and travel bookings. Corridra handles the agreed commercial work, including meetings and factory visits.

What happens after I send a request?

A Corridra team member reviews the request and asks for any missing details. If the team can take on the assignment, you’ll receive a proposed scope and quote.
