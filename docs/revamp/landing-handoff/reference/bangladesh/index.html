Before you start

How much does it cost?

Corridra quotes for the agreed scope. You review the quote before deciding.

Can I keep my current agent or trade-fair arrangements?

Yes. Corridra can work alongside your agent, supplier, chamber or fair to find relevant companies, check fit and track follow-up.

What happens after I send a request?

A team member reviews the request and asks for what they need. If Corridra can take it on, you receive a scope and quote.
